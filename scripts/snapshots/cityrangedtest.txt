a bottle: thrown 6 tiles the way faced, or onto the nearest of the dead ahead; heard 8 tiles round where it breaks by those not after anyone; a wall in the face: refused, the bottle kept
a molotov: nine tiles burn 240 steps; one of the dead standing in it is put down in 37 steps, a brute, three times as long; one coming through it loses 24; the thrower is burnt 4 every 10 steps in it and not out of it; 36 tiles at the most; gone on leaving the district or falling; stopped by a barred door; it burns on while the player sleeps, and wakes them in it
the crossbow: one out of arm's reach and within 7 tiles ahead is shot (26 a bolt: two for a shambler), every 30 steps, heard 2 tiles off; one in reach is struck as before; nobody to shoot, or no bolt: the blow; one in reach behind doesn't take the shot; no energy spent; 23 of 40 bolts found again; broken after 40 shots
made: a molotov by hand, the crossbow and three bolts at a workbench; saves: what burns is kept and plays on the same, none from before, 9 damaged ones loaded as none
