# Decisions: Jobs, money and favours

Jobs and the Work Period, cash, merchants, favours, the vending machine. Where the design document (GDD) and `tech_plan.md` disagree, or say nothing, what was decided: the GDD wins unless a row says why not. Moved word for word from `PLAN.md` §2 on 2026-11-06, one file per area, rows in the order they were written. A new conflict, or a change to a decision, goes into its area's file in the same commit.

| Jobs (P6) | 6 jobs, quotas, "fired at 15:00" | L1: **laundry $30 (4 loads), janitor $35 (6 puddles), kitchen $40 (4 trays), woodshop $50 (3 stools), mailman $55 (3 letters)**, INT 20/25/30/40/50. The wage is paid **the moment the quota is met**; an unmet quota at 15:00 costs the job (no heat). The player **starts in the laundry**; the woodshop starts open. Work items (dirty and clean laundry, raw food, trays, stools, letters) go back at 15:00. Timber taken at the woodshop is real timber. A station's step takes the action button over the furniture's container while there's a step to do there (with a load already in hand the next press opens the bin, quota or no quota; once the quota is done, the first press does) |
| Job rooms | Cyan-key doors (kitchen, laundry, mailroom, janitor) | Job-room doors **stand open during the Work Period** (12:00–15:00), and stay open while anyone is inside or in the doorway, so nobody is ever shut in. Since v2.30 guards mind anyone in a work room not theirs during those hours (see Jobs and the Work Period below) |
| Losing a job (inmates) | "Beat them up or block them" | An inmate holding a job that is **knocked out during the Work Period**, or never reaches its room, loses it at 15:00. A Work Period excused by a lockdown (or the player out cold) costs nobody their job. A job left open is filled by an inmate **two mornings later** (the player has until then to apply). Applying: at the job board, Intellect checked; taken after 12:00, it counts **from tomorrow** |
| Cash | "Looting small change" | Cash is a number, not an item. Change turns up at 08:00 in some inmate desks ($1–8) and pockets ($1–6), taken on opening them. Spent at merchants and as gifts |
| Merchants | 3 inmates, free time, up to 50% off | Cells 2, 6, 7; up to **4 items** from the merchant table (exactly the items whose source is "merchant"), topped up at 08:00; open in **free time** only; price = value × (1 − discount), discount **0 at opinion 50, 50% at 100**; at opinion 25 or less they refuse to deal. **2.36:** their "$" shows whenever they're open and would deal, sold out or not |
| Favors | 4 kinds, $20–50, +15 | Up to **3 on offer** ("!" over the asker), refreshed at 08:00; up to 3 taken on at once. Scavenge (bring an everyday item), retrieve (the item is put in the thief's pockets when it's taken on), beatdown (done when the target is knocked out), roll call distraction (done by landing a blow during a roll call; a guard who sees it still sounds the alarm). Handed in by talking to the asker. **2.36:** one taken on and not done can be let go (`TALK.DROP`, −5 opinion with the asker); a scavenge favor taken on puts what it asks for in a desk not the asker's when none shows one (no draw; `Sim.rules235` for older logs) |

| The vending machine (v2.32.0, the user's request; `Sim.rules231` and `World.plainVending` for older logs) | Not in the GDD: a vending machine stood in four canteens as decor. §7's items: Toothpaste $5, Talcum Powder $8 (the putty's two halves) | **One in every campaign canteen** (`PrisonDef.vending`: its tile, fronting south, and its queue's tiles; Stalag Flucht's new, the others where the decor stood; the Training Block none), a `vending` prop, solid from 2.32 only. **Goods** (`content/vending.ts` `GOODS`, rows A1–C1): chocolate $10 (+25 energy), soda $6 (+12), coffee $8 (+18), crisps $5 (+10 health; new items with `food`), toothpaste $7, soap $7, talcum $11 (crafting); dearer than the GDD's values and the kiosk, so nothing's made selling them on, and talcum one a day. **Stock:** each row's `stock`, refilled at the prison day's start, `DELIVERY_EXTRA` more on a delivery day; inmates take from the same rows. **Buying** (`EXT.VEND`: open, close, buy, thump, wait, cut): paid up front, `COIL_STEPS` (1.5 s), into the hand; refused with nothing taken when sold out, the pockets full, short of cash, dark (a power cut) or shut (a lockdown, a drill, which also close the card). **Jams:** one buy in `JAM_ODDS` (7), never a run's first (`jamsAt`, by `roll`); 1 to `THUMPS_MAX` thumps (noise `THUMP_NOISE`); a guard who sees it: `THUMP_HEAT` (+3%) once a jam, never past the chase's threshold, its own `vend` event, not counted as spotted. Walked away, the money's gone and the stuck item stays till the morning for whoever thumps it. **Inmates** (by `roll`, no draws from the state's RNG): in each free time, 3 in 4 want something at one of three rushes; up to `LINE_MAX` (3) queue on the machine's tiles, buy food or drink (`FANCIED`) and eat it nearby (`Inmate.snack`); a merchant, the fixer and the librarian stay put; a jam they meet, they thump, and one time in 3 give up on; called away mid-buy (a film, a talent night, a drill, a lockdown), what they paid for goes with them. **The queue:** someone there first, the player waits (a place in line) or pushes in (`CUT_OPINION` with each passed, `CUT_SWING`/`HOSTILE_SWING` that the one pushed back swings); with someone actually waiting, `VEND_TURN_STEPS` (15 s) without buying passes the turn, counted from their arrival and restarted by a jam or a thump. **The button:** the machine faced from its front or sides comes ahead of a queuer beside it; something on the floor in reach, with room for it, first; a tray in both hands is refused with a reason. A blow ends the card. **Eating:** any `food` item by the tool button, refused at a full bar (crisps: at full health). |
