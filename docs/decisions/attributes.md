# Decisions: Energy, health and attributes

The bars, training at the gym and in the library, decay. Where the design document (GDD) and `tech_plan.md` disagree, or say nothing, what was decided: the GDD wins unless a row says why not. Moved word for word from `PLAN.md` §2 on 2026-11-06, one file per area, rows in the order they were written. A new conflict, or a change to a decision, goes into its area's file in the same commit.

| Topic | tech_plan.md | Decision (GDD) |
|---|---|---|
| Attribute decay | Not covered | **−1 per day** for each attribute that isn’t trained, never below the starting 40 (see P6 below) |
| Energy | Not covered | **Below 10% energy, no tools and no training**. Restores: bed +100, meal +35, shower +2/s, chair +1.5/s, chocolate +15. 2.28: the Lights Out row |
| Energy and HP bars | P2 in §5 map | Nothing spends Energy or HP before tools (P4) and combat (P5), so both bars arrive **with what uses them**; meals, showers and chocolate restore Energy from then on |
| Energy | Bar from P4 | **0–100**: tools spend it (GDD per-tool cost); back +1 every 2 game minutes, +1 a minute asleep, +35 at a meal, +20 at the showers, +25 for a bar of chocolate (eaten with the use button) |
| Training (P6) | Rhythm minigame; +2; 12% / 10% energy | Weights (STR) and treadmill (SPD) by the action button; library study from the **shelf's panel** ("Study a book"; since 2.31, the library's own row). A set: **alternate left and right presses** (A/D or ←/→; two pads on touch) fill a bar that drains when idle; a full bar is **+2** and costs 12 energy (study 10). Under 10 energy: no training. 2.29: the Exercise row |
| Attribute decay | −1 a day if neglected | An attribute **not trained the day before slips 1 at 08:00, never below its starting 40**: trained gains fade, the baseline that recipes and jobs are tuned for stays. Speed moves the player too: **184 + 3/5 per point over 40** (220 at 100; a chasing guard runs 196) |
| The library and Intellect (v2.31.0, the user's request; `Sim.rules230` and `World.plainBooks` for older logs) | §3.2: "Interact with Bookshelves in the Library to read study books (consumes 10% Energy per session for +2 Intellect)"; §8: a recipe is recorded once crafted | Still +2 for 10 energy. **A set is read:** a subject taken from the shelf's panel (`EXT`-free: `OP.TRAIN` a 0, b the subject), the book in hand only in the library, read on a free chair (the button's first call there, before anyone to talk to) or at the shelf with none free; a marker along the page, 6 key words a page (`READ_GAPS`), one press each within `READ_WINDOW`, `READ_HIT` of the bar, a stray press spending the next; the bar fills by itself every `READ_FILL_EVERY` steps. About 5 s kept up with, 20 s untouched. **2.34 (the user's request; `Sim.rules233` for older logs):** the marker at half the speed (`READ_PACE`), stopping at each key word: lit from `READ_LEAD` steps before, it waits up to `READ_WAIT` (`state.readWait`; the marker's place held by moving `readFrom` on), then goes on, missed; a press while it's lit underlines it, one with nothing lit spends the next; the bar fills by the clock. About 8 s kept up with. **A daily limit, not in the GDD (the user's choice):** `READ_SETS_A_DAY` 6, then refused till 08:00; courses, checkers and the quiz teach on top. **Subjects, not in the GDD (the user's choice):** each new mark's tip from the subject read (else the simplest of any, so no mark goes for nothing); inmates' tips as ever. **Inmates:** a shelf tile then a chair, with a book, in free time (`HANGOUTS_READ`); about 2 to 3 in a library. **The librarian:** by cell in `leisure.librarian`, a chair of their own (`deskChair`) in free time, keeps any job, suggests a subject in talk. A blow, a lockdown, a knockout end it; a step shuts the book and leaves it in hand. |
| Exercise and training (v2.29.0, the user's request; `Sim.rules228` for older logs) | §3.2: "a rhythmic progress-bar minigame (alternating keys rapidly to fill the bar before draining 12% Energy for +2 Strength)"; "Weight Bench or Push-Up Mat", "Treadmill or Speed Bag" | **A set in time** (`SET_BEATS`): left and right in turn, a dot crossing a line each beat, only the first press of a beat counting, for far more on the line than off it: about 4.5 s kept in time, about four times that otherwise, tapping faster gaining nothing, and the bar no longer drains, so any pace finishes. The rhythm stays; "rapidly" goes, by the user's choice. Still +2 for 12 energy (study 10), doubled when chilled, and the card now says so. **Inmates on the machines:** in the exercise hour and free time, by their cell and with no new draw two in three of those bound for the gym or the ground use a free machine, from a tile of its own (`places.machines`); never a merchant or the fixer, nor at the Training Block. **Giving way:** the button at a taken machine says "Ask for a turn"; about 2 s (`GIVE_WAY_STEPS`) and they step off and the player's on it, never refused, called off by a step away, a lockdown or the like. Someone on a machine isn't talked to; too tired or at 100, with no use for it, the button talks to them instead. **The bag (Speed) and the mat (Strength):** every gym has both; not solid, as they were as decor, and last in the prop list, so old runs walk the same map; inert in old runs (`World.plainGear`), and the floor under them digs as ever. In a small gym the machine faced wins the button. A direction held from walking up no longer steps off (`loop.ts`). |
