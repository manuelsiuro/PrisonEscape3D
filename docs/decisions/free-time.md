# Decisions: Free time and games

The side games, cards, long games, football, the tattoo parlour, stage nights. Where the design document (GDD) and `tech_plan.md` disagree, or say nothing, what was decided: the GDD wins unless a row says why not. Moved word for word from `PLAN.md` §2 on 2026-11-06, one file per area, rows in the order they were written. A new conflict, or a change to a decision, goes into its area's file in the same commit.

The inmate calls a claim its own cards (and the true cards it laid) prove false, else on the claim's exact odds as it sees them (`claimOdds`: 60% of its doubt past 20, +10 with nothing true); it lays 1–3 of its true cards as `roll()` says (the rest kept back), else a bluff of 1–3: the size of a play tells nothing. Its tell (by cell: an ear, a glance, fingers, a whistle) shows on 40% + Intellect/4 (at most 80%) of its lies and 20% of its truths.
