# Decisions: Ways out

Every escape route, the takeover, the getaways, parole and the paperwork. Where the design document (GDD) and `tech_plan.md` disagree, or say nothing, what was decided: the GDD wins unless a row says why not. Moved word for word from `PLAN.md` §2 on 2026-11-06, one file per area, rows in the order they were written. A new conflict, or a change to a decision, goes into its area's file in the same commit.
