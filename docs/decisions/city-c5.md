# Decisions: City Escape, stage C5 (Make it yours, 2.66 on)

The testers' asks of 2026-11-20 and the stage built from them ([`../phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`](../phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md)):
what the mode's design ([`../source/city_escape_design.md`](../source/city_escape_design.md)) doesn't have, or where it departs,
as it stands now. The mode's other rows are [`city.md`](city.md); split from it at 2.70, when it outgrew its size.

| Topic (version) | The design says | The decision |
|---|---|---|
| Thirst and the states (2.70) | not in the GDD (water was food); the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 5; the user's choice: thirst a meter of its own) | **In a city begun from 2.70 (`state.rev` 6), thirst is a fourth meter** (`content/survival.ts`, `sim/survival.ts`): a full bar lasts about 24 hours awake and 55 asleep; under a quarter, a slower walk; at none, a point of health every 4 minutes and no health back; it wakes a sleeper, and nobody parched goes to bed. What's drunk gives it (water 50, a rain barrel's 40, tea 30, dirty water 25, a soda 20, fish soup 20, coffee 10, fruit 8); bottled water is in fridges, shops, offices and garages, dirty water in toilets. **Five states**, with their time left under the meters: well fed (a cooked meal: health back twice as often, 6 h), refreshed (tea, coffee, a soda: energy half as fast, 3 h), rested (a night of six hours slept, woken in the morning: a blow costs half the energy, 12 h), queasy (dirty water, raw fish: hunger and thirst twice as fast, 4 h; tea or pills settle it), soaked (out in the rain without a raincoat: energy half again as fast, an hour after; a fire dries at once). **Fresh food goes off at dawn** in the pockets and the bag: half of each stack, rounded up, of fruit, vegetables, fish and cooked meals (a chest keeps it; tins, jerky, preserves keep). Claude's picks: the numbers; no thirst and none of the states in a city under way. Measured on 20 cities, the scavenger bot falls 2.8 a week (none to thirst, parched 1 in 100 of its minutes) against 2.75 without; the story bot with the dead away, on 24 cities, gets out more often (36 and 33 of 36 against 31 and 33) and about a day later (the helicopter by day 5.9 and 5.3 against 4.5 and 5.2, the metro 6.5 and 5.6 against 4.3 and 4.9), parched 11 to 23 in 100 of its minutes: it searches as it goes, water first only when thirsty. |
| Weather (2.70) | not in the GDD; the testers' asks (batch 5; the user's choice: weather) | **Each day's weather from the city's seed** (no draw: day 1 clear; then clear 45, rain 20, fog 15, heat 12, storm 8 in 100), from 06:00 to the next, said at dawn (a banner, Dispatch with the radio) and beside the day on the HUD. **Rain and storm**: barrels fill, planters are watered, the dead hear half as far, a player out of doors without a raincoat is soaked; drawn as rain round the player, a storm's lightning flashing. **Fog** till 11:00: the dead see three tiles at the most (drawn as the boat's night's fog). **Heat**: thirst half again as fast. None in the tunnel, none in a city begun before 2.70. Claude's picks: the shares; the weather a whole day long; the dead's hearing halved for the scream too. |
| Water, the garden and fishing (2.70) | not in the GDD; the testers' asks (batch 5) | **A pond or the sea beside** (after a window or the fence there): the button drinks (dirty: queasy), or, a fishing rod in the pockets and thirst over half, casts (8 seconds stood still with the rod in the pockets, heard two tiles off; a fish one time in two, two at the docks' sea; the rod a cast more worn; no draw: the seed and the step). **Three pieces from manuals**: a planter (seeds planted from its card, watered once a day with water or by the rain; three days watered, then harvested for two or three vegetables, herbs and a seed back), a rain barrel (3 drinks a day of rain, 8 at most: drunk from, or a bottle filled), a smoker (jerky made beside it). Their state is worked out from the days' weather when looked at (nothing runs in a district not entered). **Cooking**: boiled water (dirty water on a fire or a stove), herbal tea, roast vegetables, fish soup, preserves; a herbal poultice stops a wound bleeding. In a city begun before 2.70 the three manuals' recipes and pieces don't appear (nothing there would make them work). Claude's picks: hens and eggs wait for batch 6; fishing as a timed cast, no tug to time. |
| Manuals and the workshop (2.69) | not in the GDD; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 4) | **Three manuals** (`MANUALS`), found where they'd be kept from revision 5 (weapons: garages, warehouses, factories, the police; sewing: shops, the mall, a house's shelves; tricks: the rangers' lodge, shops, offices) and **from the second Rush held, one not yet read in the supplies**: read from their card, each teaches its recipes (6, 4 and 3) and is put away; read again, "you know this one". A recipe still to learn shows as **"?" with where its manual may lie**. In a city begun before 2.69 they are known. **The workshop**, built from a toolkit found (garages, factories, warehouses, the boathouse), 4 planks, 2 scrap and 2 nails: a workbench too, and the place for the steel crossbow and scrap armour; taken down, its toolkit back whole, never in a repair's cost. **Mending at a bench**: a pocket's worn weapon or tool for tape and scrap, armour for cloth and tape, half its full blows back. Upgrades take what they improve, and the new one comes whole. **Ranged weapons by what they shoot** (`shoots`): the crossbow as before, the steel crossbow (40, 9 tiles), the slingshot (scrap: 12, 6 tiles, quiet; knocks a runner down); the one that hurts most, with its ammunition carried, shoots. Claude's picks: three manuals now (the others come with batch 5's gardening, fishing, cooking); the toolkit found only; upgrades whole. |
| Armour, bags and tricks (2.69) | not in the GDD; the testers' asks (batch 4) | **Armour worn** (`state.armour`, from its card; under the corner map, its card with Take off): a share off each bite, a bite of wear each (padded jacket 25% over 30 bites, sports pads 20%/25, scrap armour 45%/50 and a walk 6% slower, riot gear 50%/60 at the police station, a camouflage poncho: seen a tile less awake, never under two); at nothing it falls apart, said; kept on through a fall. On the player: riot gear as the guards' uniform and cap, the poncho as the ghillie cloak, the rest a vest of its colour. **A bag on the back** (`state.bag`; a satchel 4 slots, a hiking pack 8): a box of its own opened from its button, **its things counting and spent for making and building**, eaten and fought with only from the pockets; not taken off or swapped for a smaller while it holds more; on a fall its things go on the pile, the bag stays on. **Tricks**: a smoke bomb thrown (a cloud of two tiles' reach for 15 seconds: the dead caught in it are blind 15 seconds, one after the player loses them, and nobody in it is seen), a noisemaker set down (rings 30 seconds later for 10, calling the dead from 14 tiles), a flare thrown (`LIGHT_KIND.FLARE`: a fire's reach for 30 game minutes; it cooks nothing). Smoke and noisemakers stay in their district. Claude's picks: the counts; the raincoat waits for batch 5's weather; the bag's things not usable from it. |
| Breaking things (2.66) | §4.5 names the crowbar for "locked doors, crates" and nothing broken; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 1) | **Furniture, cars and wrecks break up, and an ordinary building's inner walls with a sledgehammer.** A blow every `BLOW.every` steps standing beside it, each heard (7 tiles on wood, 10 on metal, 12 a wall), costing a blow's energy and wearing what strikes: the best thing carried for the stuff (`content/salvage.ts`: fists and blades only wood; a bat both; an axe wood four times as fast as fists; a sledgehammer metal fastest; the crowbar both, unworn; tired, half). Begun by the blow's button with none of the dead in reach and a piece faced, or the open piece's Break; a wall only by the action button (a blow at the air never starts on one); walking off or a blow at the dead leaves the work. Done: furniture gone for good (its tiles walked on), what it held and what it is made of in a pile where it stood, the pile opened; a car or a wreck stripped (it stays, emptied; its metal at the player's feet; an alarmed one sounds); a wall a way through, the district put up again. Never: the story's furniture or a note's, a survivor's building, a station (workbench, generator), the landmarks' pieces, an outer wall, a landmark's or a survivor's walls. The **axe** (9, an edge) and the **sledgehammer** (11, knocks down) are weapons too, made at a bench; found only in a city begun from 2.66 (`state.rev` 2: garages, warehouses, the factory, the rangers', the boathouse). Kept by district in the save (`broken`, `stripped`, `holes`); a load ends work under way. |
| Building a base (2.67) | §4.4: a shelter is a building claimed, its doors barred; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 2) | **With a hammer carried (a plank, scrap and tape, by hand; found from `state.rev` 3), eleven pieces go up on the tile faced** (`content/build.ts`), three seconds' hammering heard seven tiles off: a barricade (60), plank and metal walls (120, 250: they hide the player too), a door (100: the player walks through it, the dead don't), a chest (12 slots), a camp bed (slept in, home as any bed), a workbench (works as any), a campfire (a light of its own, `LIGHT_KIND.CAMP`: three tiles, six hours, four more a plank; it cooks), a table, a chair and a rug (walked over). Walls and doors stand across the way faced. **Never** in the tunnel, within two tiles of a checkpoint's street ends, beside a survivor or at a way out's tile (or the pier's end), on anything or anyone; 120 a district. **The dead after the player go round what's built; with no way round, they take the way through it and pound the first piece** (a point of its health every 10 steps, a brute's four; **since 2.68 the cheapest way, each piece weighing by the blows it takes**: a barricade gives before a plank wall, a plank wall before a metal one, and no step slips diagonally past a piece); one that isn't after anyone goes round. A piece's card: its health, Repair (whole again for half of what it took, for the share worn away: never dearer than a new one), Take down (half back, less as worn; a chest's things on the ground). **Comfort round a sleeper**: what's built within six tiles (a camp bed 3, a campfire burning 2, a chest, a table, a chair, a rug 1 each) and a light burning 1: a Hideout from 3 gives energy back asleep a quarter faster and health every 3 minutes, a Home from 6 half again faster and every 2. Barred street doors and shelters are as before. A solid piece stops sight only if it's a wall or a door (a table, a chest, a barricade don't hide the player); the tile the player stands on never hides them from a bite (2.67, the review: a doorway, built or barred, was a place no bite reached). Nothing on a home tile, the district's own spot or furniture walked over. Kept by district in the save (`built`). Claude's picks: pieces only where nothing stands (furniture is broken first, 2.66); the shortest way through, not the weakest piece (2.67; since 2.68 the weakest: walls then make a door of the weak point, where the traps are); the hammer the one tool asked. |
| The Rush (2.68) | not in the GDD; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 3; the user's choice: every third night, announced, no sleeping through it) | **In a city begun from 2.68 (`state.rev` 4), nights 3, 6, 9… are the Rush** (`content/rush.ts`, `sim/rush.ts`): said at 06:00 and 19:00 (a banner, Dispatch with the radio found; the HUD counts down to 22:00), **no sleep from 20:00 to 05:00** ("Not tonight. Listen."). **Four waves, 22:00, midnight, 02:00, 04:00**, put down in the streets out of sight: 3 + one more each Rush, each wave, each step of the district's danger (14 at most); a brute in the second and last waves from the second Rush, a bloater in each from the third. They are **after the player wherever they are**: each looks where the player is every two seconds and comes the way round, else through what's built and the barred doors (pounded), and no light turns them back. **Crossed into another district, every one of the Rush's about follows** (put down out of sight). None in the tunnel; none while a way out's beacon or generator calls the district's dead (its night is the Rush enough): no wave there, nothing to hold. **Held at 05:00, if a wave came: supplies dropped on the player's tile**, better each Rush held (10, 17, then 23 things: planks, nails, scrap, wire, food, bandages, bolts, a medkit). Fallen: as any fall. Claude's picks: the counts and supplies; no manual or armour in them until batch 4 brings them; a city under way keeps no Rush. Measured (`cityrushtest`, 22 cities a set-up): stood in the street with a bat, never held; in a pocket of plank walls with spikes and wire before its barricade, the first held in 18, the second in 5; with metal walls, a spear and five bandages, 22, 14, and the third 7. The scavenger bot, never building or hiding, falls on about 38 Rush nights in 100 (2.9 and 3.1 falls a week on 80 cities, 1.1 and 1.2 without). |

| A base left (2.68) | not in the GDD; the design (batch 2's "a base left") | **Back in a district after nights away (each midnight passed away from it a night), each piece the dead can walk up to** (on or beside a tile they reach from the streets) **has taken the nights**: 10 a night and 6 more for each step of the danger, half again lit, a Rush's night three times, a piece walked over half (a few points more or less by tile, no draw); at none it is gone. Said on arrival ("It held: 3 pieces damaged"). Pieces walled in are untouched. The step each district was left is saved (`left`). Since 2.68 a sleeper in a base (pieces built within six tiles) is looked for by the night's siege as one behind boards is, the way through what's built, and a light by them adds 3 in 100 (a shelter's odds are as before). Building and its consequences work in every city; only the Rush waits for a city begun from 2.68. |
