# Decisions: City Escape, stage C5 (Make it yours, 2.66 on)

The testers' asks of 2026-11-20 and the stage built from them ([`../phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`](../phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md)):
what the mode's design ([`../source/city_escape_design.md`](../source/city_escape_design.md)) doesn't have, or where it departs,
as it stands now. The mode's other rows are [`city.md`](city.md); split from it at 2.70, when it outgrew its size.
