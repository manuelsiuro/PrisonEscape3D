# Decisions: City Escape

The second mode (v2.46 on). Its design is [`../source/city_escape_design.md`](../source/city_escape_design.md);
a row here is where the build departs from it or settles what it leaves open, as it stands now.

| Topic (version) | The design says | The decision |
|---|---|---|
| A mode beside the prisons (2.46) | "a new mode accessible from the main menu … while preserving the original game mode" | **Its own simulation, no prison game in progress** while it runs (`play.side`), in `src/city/`, fetched when first opened. Nothing added to the prisons' lists or state; the title's button is the one visible change, and it is **hidden without `?city`** until stage C4 (the user's choice, 2026-11-13). |
| The city's size (2.46) | "several 64x64 maps with transition points" | **3 × 3 districts** (the user's choice), each its own 64 × 64 `World` built when entered; one is in memory at a time. A crossing is a checkpoint: stepping on a street's two border tiles puts the player a tile inside the neighbour, on the same street. |
| What a district is drawn from (2.46) | §2: blocks, buildings, landmarks | **The prisons' own tiles and furniture**: walls, floors, doors and props as the legend has them (`rows` in the map's characters), so the view draws them with no new tile kind; streets are `ROAD`, pavements and car parks `ASPHALT` (an indoor kind would be roofed). What stands on the streets is the city's own (`DECO`), solid through `World.parked`. Street doors are yellow: open to all. |
| Lanes (2.46) | §2: streets meet at the border | **Three lanes each way per grid column and row**, at 11, 30 and 49 tiles ±2, two tiles wide; a district has every lane a checkpoint uses and three in four of the others, two at least each way. |
| The clock (2.46) | §3: a day is 24 real minutes | The prisons' pace (20 steps a game minute), from **06:30 on day 1**. Night is the prisons' (21:00 to 05:00, `NIGHT_FROM`/`NIGHT_TO`). |
| The map's fog (2.46) | §2: districts fill in as they are walked | **A disc of 7 tiles** round the player, through walls (a map of where they have been, not of what they saw); a bit a tile per district in the save. |
| Saving (2.46) | not said | **One city in progress**, in its own slot (`pe3d.city.save`), written on entering a district, on pausing and when the page is hidden or closed; Reset progress wipes it. No run log or replay. The Intake is asked before a first city, as before a first prison. |
| Death's two modes (2.46) | §4.6 | **Chosen on the new city's card and stored** (`MODE`); nothing kills until stage C2. |
| Items (2.47) | §4.5: "the city's own items and recipes kept apart from the prisons' lists" | **A list of its own** (`CITY_ITEMS`, append only), each with a shape from the item models and its own colours; its pockets are its own (`Stack`: an item and a count), not the prisons' `ItemState`: simpler, and nothing of a prison's contraband, uses or slots applies. The icons are taken by the prisons' studio (`render/itemIcons.ts`: `photos`). |
| Searching (2.47) | §3: "search homes, shops, cars and bins" | **Furniture only** for now (cars and the street's wrecks with C2b). A piece's contents are drawn when first opened, from the seed, the district and the piece's number: the same in every play of a city, and nothing is stored for what was never opened. |
| Sleep and home (2.47) | §4.4: shelters claimed, one of them home | Until shelters (C2c), **any searched bed can be slept in, and the last one is home**. Sleep is refused by day unless tired (energy under 60), and while starving. |
| Hunger's pace (2.47) | §4.1: "falls all day" | A full bar lasts **about 30 hours awake** (55 thousandths a minute), half that pace asleep; a scavenger bot eats about three things a day and finds about four. Running doesn't cost more yet (there's no run). |
| A Survivor's fall (2.47) | §4.6: "the night's horde has grown by a night" | Wakes at home at **06:30 the next morning** with 40 health, at least 15 hunger (a fall is no meal: the review's exploit) and 60 energy; **each fall counts as a night more** for the night's dead. |
| The dead, one district at a time (2.47) | §4.2: "at night they spawn at the dark places and roam" | **Only the district the player is in has any**, put down afresh at each entry from the hour, the day and the district's danger: a checkpoint is a way out of a chase, and nothing is simulated or saved for the other eight. Runners and brutes come with stage C3. |
| A bite's wind-up (2.47) | §4.2: "a grab, then bites while held: breaking free costs energy" | No grab: **a lunge of nine steps before each first bite, begun again when struck**. Measured on the bot: without it one shambler cost two bites to put down with fists and a week cost 25 falls; with it, none and 1.7. |
| Bodies (2.47) | not said | **Nobody walks through the dead**: the player's step into one isn't taken (a doorway is held, and a room can be shut in on them), nor one of a chasing pack's into another of it; the dead wandering pass each other (two face to face in an alley would stand there for good). |
| The blow (2.47) | §4.5: weapons "wearing out" | **The best weapon in the pockets strikes, with no wear yet** (wear comes with crafting, C2c). Fists 6, a bat 18, a knife 22 against a shambler's 30. |
| Sleeping before shelters (2.47) | §4.4 | A sleeper is seen only from two tiles, and a bite wakes them. Nights are meant to be broken until doors can be barred (C2c). |
