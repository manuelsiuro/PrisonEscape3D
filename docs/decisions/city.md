# Decisions: City Escape

The second mode (v2.46 on). Its design is [`../source/city_escape_design.md`](../source/city_escape_design.md);
a row here is where the build departs from it or settles what it leaves open, as it stands now.

| Topic (version) | The design says | The decision |
|---|---|---|
| A mode beside the prisons (2.46) | "a new mode accessible from the main menu … while preserving the original game mode" | **Its own simulation, no prison game in progress** while it runs (`play.side`), in `src/city/`, fetched when first opened. Nothing added to the prisons' lists or state; the title's button is the one visible change, and it is **hidden without `?city`** until stage C4 (the user's choice, 2026-11-13). |
| The city's size (2.46) | "several 64x64 maps with transition points" | **3 × 3 districts** (the user's choice), each its own 64 × 64 `World` built when entered; one is in memory at a time. A crossing is a checkpoint: stepping on a street's two border tiles puts the player a tile inside the neighbour, on the same street. |
| What a district is drawn from (2.46) | §2: blocks, buildings, landmarks | **The prisons' own tiles and furniture**: walls, floors, doors and props as the legend has them (`rows` in the map's characters), so the view draws them with no new tile kind; streets are `ROAD`, pavements and car parks `ASPHALT` (an indoor kind would be roofed). What stands on the streets is the city's own (`DECO`), solid through `World.parked`. Street doors are yellow: open to all. |
| Lanes (2.46) | §2: streets meet at the border | **Three lanes each way per grid column and row**, at 11, 30 and 49 tiles ±2, two tiles wide; a district has every lane a checkpoint uses and three in four of the others, two at least each way. |
| The clock (2.46) | §3: a day is 24 real minutes | The prisons' pace (20 steps a game minute), from **06:30 on day 1**. Night is the prisons' (21:00 to 05:00, `NIGHT_FROM`/`NIGHT_TO`). |
| The map's fog (2.46) | §2: districts fill in as they are walked | **A disc of 7 tiles** round the player, through walls (a map of where they have been, not of what they saw); a bit a tile per district in the save. |
| Saving (2.46) | not said | **One city in progress**, in its own slot (`pe3d.city.save`), written on entering a district, on pausing and when the page is hidden or closed; Reset progress wipes it. No run log or replay. The Intake is asked before a first city, as before a first prison. |
| Death's two modes (2.46) | §4.6 | **Chosen on the new city's card and stored** (`MODE`). Since 2.47 hunger and the dead kill: a Survivor wakes at home (the row below), Hardcore loses the city and its save. |
| Items (2.47) | §4.5: "the city's own items and recipes kept apart from the prisons' lists" | **A list of its own** (`CITY_ITEMS`, append only), each with a shape from the item models and its own colours; its pockets are its own (`Stack`: an item and a count), not the prisons' `ItemState`: simpler, and nothing of a prison's contraband, uses or slots applies. The icons are taken by the prisons' studio (`render/itemIcons.ts`: `photos`). |
| Searching (2.47) | §3: "search homes, shops, cars and bins" | **Furniture, and since 2.54 the cars and wrecks left in the streets** (a box each, searched from beside it; one draw, half empty: fuel, batteries and wire in a car, scrap in a wreck). Claude's picks for cars: **one car in six still has its alarm**, shown beforehand by a red light blinking on its roof; searched, it calls the dead that aren't after anyone from twelve tiles round, each minute for twenty; it is a trap to leave alone or a lure to set off. A piece's contents are drawn when first opened, from the seed, the district and the piece's number: the same in every play of a city, and nothing is stored for what was never opened. |
| Sleep and home (2.47) | §4.4: shelters claimed, one of them home | Until shelters (C2c), **any searched bed can be slept in, and the last one is home** (before the first night, home is where the city began, by the bus). Sleep is refused by day unless tired (energy under 60), and while starving. Since 2.64 a bed with things stored in it is slept in from its open box's Sleep (`COP.SLEEP`): a stash no longer costs the bed. **Since 2.65 a bed by day is a rest till 20:00**, tired or not (home, but not the first act's night in a bed) (the button says so; a push on the stick gets up), and a sleeper with the boat made ready is woken at 22:30 for its tide: a night's wait was sixteen real minutes walked through. |
| Hunger's pace (2.47) | §4.1: "falls all day" | A full bar lasts **about 30 hours awake** (55 thousandths a minute), half that pace asleep; a scavenger bot eats about three things a day and finds about four. Running doesn't cost more yet (there's no run). |
| A Survivor's fall (2.47) | §4.6: "the night's horde has grown by a night" | Wakes at home at **06:30 the next morning** with 40 health, at least 15 hunger (a fall is no meal: the review's exploit) and 60 energy; **each fall counts as a night more** for the night's dead. Since 2.64 the fall is **a card waited for** (where they woke, where the pile is, the map a button away), not a banner of five seconds that players missed. |
| The dead, one district at a time (2.47) | §4.2: "at night they spawn at the dark places and roam" | **Only the district the player is in has any**, put down afresh at each entry from the hour, the day and the district's danger: a checkpoint is a way out of a chase, and nothing is simulated or saved for the other eight. Runners and brutes come with stage C3. **Since 2.65 ground cleared stays cleared until the next 21:00** (the user's choice, 2026-11-19): those put down in a place since the last dusk are taken off its count at each entry (indoors first), a count a place in the save (`cleared`); the night's trickle still fills a district to its full count. Before, a checkpoint crossed twice put every one back. |
| A bite's wind-up (2.47) | §4.2: "a grab, then bites while held: breaking free costs energy" | No grab: **a lunge of nine steps before each first bite, begun again when struck**. Measured on the bot: without it one shambler cost two bites to put down with fists and a week cost 25 falls; with it, none and 1.7. |
| Bodies (2.47) | not said | **Nobody walks through the dead**: the player's step into one isn't taken (a doorway is held, and a room can be shut in on them), nor one of a chasing pack's into another of it; the dead wandering pass each other (two face to face in an alley would stand there for good). |
| The blow (2.47) | §4.5: weapons "wearing out" | **The best weapon in the pockets strikes, with no wear yet** (wear comes with crafting, C2c). Fists 6, a bat 18, a knife 22 against a shambler's 30 (since 2.65 the weapon that strikes is chosen for the one struck: the row on wear). Since 2.64, **with none ahead in reach, the nearest in reach on any side is struck and the player turns to it** (one at the side bit unanswered: nothing showed which way the player faced). |
| Sleeping before shelters (2.47) | §4.4 | A sleeper is seen only from two tiles, and a bite wakes them. Nights are meant to be broken until doors can be barred (C2c). |
| The first day's things (2.64) | §4.5: weapons and boards found | **A city begun from 2.64 (`state.rev` 1; a save without it is 0 and keeps its draws) holds what its first nights need:** a bat (30 blows), two planks and nails with the radio, in its furniture; planks and nails on houses' shelves; bats in garages, warehouses and cars. All its start district searched, boards can be made in 30 cities of 30 (8 before), a bat found in 30 (16). Claude's picks: the radio's owner meant to hold out; a house's other furniture keeps its food. |
| Recipes (2.47; three taught since 2.48) | §4.5: weapons "crafted and wearing out"; cooking on a fire | **Eight recipes, all known from the start** (finding the parts is the game): three by hand, three at a workbench, two with a camp stove carried (a fire barrel and light wait for a later slice). The crossbow, the molotov and the noise lure wait for a way to aim (stage C3). |
| Wear (2.47) | §4.5 | **A weapon's count in its pocket is its blows left**, so a weapon never stacks and a save needs no new field; it wears only when a blow lands. One weapon strikes, with its own reach. **Since 2.65, with no button to choose, the one that suits the one struck** (`armFor`): of those that reach it, one that puts it down for good (a blade, or any on a crawler or a bloater), else one that knocks it down, else the strongest (then the longer); one lying down is finished by a blade if one is carried; the reach looked in is the longest carried. Before, the strongest always struck: a knife carried with a nailed bat never did, nor a spear against a bloater from out of its burst. The hand shows the one that struck last. |
| Shelters (2.47) | §4.4: "any building with a bed can be claimed once cleared: bar its doors, light it, stash loot in it" | **A building is a shelter when every street door is barred** with boards (a recipe); no bed is needed and nothing checks it is cleared: one of the dead shut in with the player is the player's problem. The player passes their own barred door; shamblers never break one (brutes will, C3). **Since 2.58 boards are not a certain night**: from the third night, each hour asleep in a shelter, a chance (2 in 100 a night past the second, 8 at most) that one of the dead within 16 tiles finds the door and pounds it; the sleeper is woken at once, the boards give 15 seconds later, a light on its way turns it back. |
| The kit (2.48) | §4.5: "parts for the ways out, one or two per district" among the items | **The story's things are not items**: a kit of their own (a bit each in the state), never in the eight pockets, never dropped, kept through a Survivor's fall. Each lies in one piece of furniture chosen from the seed and is found on its first search, asked for yet or not. |
| The radio (2.48) | §5: "a hand-cranked radio in the first shelter"; act 2, "crank the radio; fetch what Dispatch asks (batteries, an aerial)" | In the **nearest house to the start with a bed**, in its furniture. It is heard as soon as found; it **mends itself once the aerial and two batteries are in hand** (the batteries leave the pockets, said by a toast): no cranking, no step to forget. Since 2.64 **the first act lists the batteries and the aerial as soon as the radio is found** (the bed last by day, first from 20:00): the first day was a wait for night with nothing on the list. Mended before the first sleep, the story goes from the first act to the third. |
| Scouting and choosing (2.48) | §5, act 3: "Dispatch names three ways out; scout each" | A way out is scouted by **walking into its landmark**, counted whenever it happens; **one scouted is enough to choose**, in the journal, and another may be chosen until one is set going. The things found for a way not taken stay in the kit. |
| What the ways take (2.48) | §5: fuel and "a torch that lasts" among the parts | **Three kit things each**, none from the pockets: the boat's fuel is a drum at the filling station, the metro's light a ranger's lamp (a found torch is rare, and pocket fuel is spent on the stove). The flare gun and the gate key card lie in the police armoury, **locked until the fence's key** (below). |
| The helicopter's night (2.48) | §5: "hold the pad till dawn" | The beacon is lit **after dark only** and the helicopter comes **60 game minutes later** (the row below has the waits) to whoever stands in the hospital (the roof isn't a floor of its own). Dawn isn't waited for: lit at 21:00 or at 04:00, it is the same wait. Barred doors are torn open one every 20 minutes, **anywhere in the district** (the building the player is in first): no boarded house sits the night out. |
| The metro's night (2.48) | §5: "the darkest place in the city, on foot" | The last night is the wait in the station: the generator's 90 minutes (60 since 2.58, 40 since 2.63.2), the dead coming up inside it and boards torn off as for the beacon. **Since 2.56 the tunnel is then walked, a tenth map** (place 9; before, the button at the open gates was the escape): one way three tiles wide from the stairs to a ladder, dark at any hour, the ranger's lamp its light (a dim one: seen at six tiles), **five of the dead standing along it** (eight before 2.65: unarmed, the metro was won 5 times in 32 against the other ways' 22, 13 of its losses in the tunnel; at five, 18), a few rooms to search; **since 2.65 the stairs are climbed back up** from their foot (the button there), into the station with the generator still running, the gates open and its dead called again (down again, the tunnel's come for the player: the stairs are no way out of a chase; before, there was no way back: the review's bot fell in the tunnel in 18 of the metro's 27 falls unarmed). The escape is the ladder's foot. Claude's picks: a fall down there stops the generator like any failed last night (the things kept; since 2.57 what was carried lies beside the top of the stairs); nobody's home is there; the city's map shows the player at the metro's stairs meanwhile. |
| The boat's night (2.48) | §5: "get to the pier through the fog; quiet beats strong" | The engine is fitted at any hour and stays fitted; the boat leaves from **the pier's end between 23:00 and 02:00**. With the engine in, the docks' nights are in fog: the dead see two tiles and hear twice as far, eight more of them. Since 2.58 **casting off is ten seconds stood still at the pier's end** (heard four tiles off, eight in the fog; begun again if left), and a boat made ready can be given up for another way from the journal (the engine comes out). |
| Failing a last night (2.48) | not said | Leaving the district or falling **stops the beacon or the generator and loses nothing**: the kit and the choice are kept, another night will do. A Survivor's falls still count as nights more for the dead. **Since 2.65 a checkpoint is held back once** while the beacon or the generator runs: the step isn't taken and it is said (a walk by tap stops); once the stick has been let go, another from the same tile crosses (within ten seconds; walked off, warned again). Dispatch's lines say to stay in the district. |
| Survivors (2.48) | §5: "survivors hold a shelter in three or four districts … each gives two or three errands for recipes, trade and a line of the story. One of them is another prisoner from the bus"; §4.4: "safe by nature" | **Four, standing still** on a tile of a building of theirs (the fence in the mall, the nurse in a clinic, the mechanic in a garage, the ranger in the lodge), **two errands each**. Safe: nobody harms them, and none of the dead are put down in their building (the dead may still follow the player in). The fence is the one from the bus. |
| Errands (2.48) | not said how they are taken or paid | **The action meets them** (the first errand asked) and is a word after; **their card's button hands the errand over** when its things are in the pockets or enough of the dead were put down since it was asked (a word never takes anything: the radio's batteries are safe). No accept or refuse. Three first errands teach a recipe (medkit, cleaver, trail ration). |
| The armoury's key (2.48) | §4.5: "the police station the armoury (a locked door: its key is a quest)" | No door: **the two story things in the police station are locked** until the fence's first errand (three bits of cloth) pays the key. The helicopter and the metro need it; the boat doesn't. |
| Swaps (2.48) | §5: "trade" | **Three fixed swaps a survivor**, as often as wanted, only beside them; no money and no stock. |
| Runners and brutes (2.48) | §4.2: runners from night 3, "as fast as the player, rarer, weaker"; brutes from night 5, "one per hard district … break barricades" | Runners **one arrival in eight, a little slower than the player** (168 against 184: caught only when tired, hungry or cornered); **one brute a district a night** where the danger is 2 or more, **pounding a barred door open in 80 steps** (it goes for a door only after someone it has seen: a sleeper behind boards isn't looked for). At night only, in the streets; no draw before the third night. |
| The city's records (2.48) | §7: "the prisons' achievements and stats (the city gets its own)" | **Ten feats and the ways out taken**, in a slot of their own, counted when a city is left and not before; shown on the end card and from the city's menu. The prisons' achievements never see them. |
| How to play and a tutorial (2.49) | roadmap C4: "How to play's topics and pictures, a short tutorial" | **A tab of its own** in How to play (nine topics), and **seven one-time tips** in place of a lesson: the city has no scripted day to teach on, and the first act's list already leads. |
| The finish's look and sound (2.49) | roadmap C4: "the palette and dressing (overgrowth, dust, signage, the landmarks' own models), sounds and music of its own" | **Awnings by use, weeds, litter, the survivors' own models; the dead's and the radio's own sounds.** Since 2.50 **the landmarks have their own models**, render only and nowhere anyone walks (a coping, a pictogram sign without words, corner pieces; the helipad, the beacon and the helicopter, the boat at the pier, the grille over the metro's stairs). Since 2.51 **a score of its own**: E Phrygian, five moods (day, night, shelter, chase, the last stand) picked from what is happening (the last stand's only where the dead are called: the boat's, while its tide is in); the prisons' tension theme no longer plays there. |
| Shown to everyone (2.49) | the user's choice: "hidden until complete" | The four stages built, **the entry is on the title for everyone from 2.49.0**; what only a person can check (touch, a real pad, a real phone) is listed in `BACKLOG.md` §2. |
| Light and fire (2.52) | §4.3: "a lit tile is one within a light's reach (a fire barrel 4 tiles, a lamp 3, a generator's lamps a whole room). The dead will not stand in strong light unless chasing"; §4.1: light "a state the HUD shows: lit, dim or dark" | **As written, lit from the pockets**: fuel used beside a street barrel (4 tiles, 8 h; it cooks) or a generator (its building, 12 h), a lantern made and set down (3 tiles, 10 h), the torch a dim light on a battery (4 h; seen at 6 tiles behind it, not 4). Claude's picks: light stops at walls; **a brute walks through it**, and so do the dead the beacon and the metro's generator call; none is put down in it; a sleeper in it is seen at the night's four tiles (a lantern is no barred door); the dead beyond the player's light at night are drawn as shapes and eyes. Fire as a weapon came with 2.55. |
| Interiors dark by day (2.53) | §3: "the best loot is where they wait: unlit interiors"; §4.2: the dead "see 4 tiles in the dark and 7 in light", by day "only in the dark (unlit interiors, the metro, sewers) and a few stragglers in the open" | **Daylight comes three tiles' walk in at a street door, never through a wall; further in it is dark by day** as the street is by night: the dead see the player there at four tiles (six behind the torch), every light works there, and by day the dead indoors are put down in the dark (one to eight dark tiles at the most, the rest indoors anywhere; at dawn they go to ground in a dark room within ten tiles' walk). Claude's picks: **a home is light throughout** (houses, the boathouse, the rangers' post: the first minutes of a city stay readable) and so is a survivor's building; a district with no dark room keeps its dead indoors anywhere; nothing of it is saved. On screen: a gloom over the dark tiles, the dead in it shapes and eyes beyond the player's own light. |
| Weapons thrown or shot (2.55) | §4.5: "the crossbow and bolts (quiet, ranged), the molotov (fire, noise), the noise lure"; §4.3: "a thrown bottle … draws the dead to where it lands" | **Thrown from the pockets (Use), shot with the blow's own button.** An empty bottle is the lure: six tiles the way faced, or onto the nearest of the dead ahead, heard eight tiles round. A molotov (a bottle, fuel, tape, by hand) burns the tile it breaks on and those round it for 240 steps: 8 health every 10 steps from each of the dead standing in it, 4 from the player. The crossbow (a plank, a pipe, wire, at a bench; three bolts from nails and scrap) shoots the nearest of the dead ahead that is **out of arm's reach** and within seven tiles (26 a bolt, heard two tiles off, 40 shots, one bolt in two found again); one in reach is struck as before. Claude's picks: no aiming other than the way faced; fire is not a light and the dead don't avoid it; it goes out when the district is left. **Since 2.65 a molotov never breaks where its fire reaches the thrower**: one of the dead at arm's length is thrown over, and with nothing further than a tile (a wall in the face) it isn't thrown, and that is said. |
| The crowbar and the bolt cutters (2.58) | §4.5: "the crowbar (locked doors, crates), bolt cutters (fences: shortcuts between blocks)" | Both were found and did nothing until 2.58. **One building in eight is chained shut** in a city begun from 2.58 (never a landmark, a survivor's, or one holding a story thing or a note): nobody walks in, none of the dead are inside (and no more elsewhere for it), its furniture holds a draw more with fewer empties (1.45 things a piece against 0.7, since 2.59); **a crowbar forces it** at any of its doors (4 s, heard), every door open for good. **Bolt cutters cut the army's fence where a street runs up to it on both sides** (5 s, heard): a crossing from then on, from both sides, on the map and in the journal's way. Neither wears out. No fence stands between blocks, so the shortcut is between districts. |
| Food never kills (2.58) | not said | Raw fish takes health down to 1 at the worst (it killed at 4 or less, a Hardcore city lost to a meal). A stew gives 75 hunger, 8 health, 15 energy (its parts 50, 2, 12); a medkit 100 health (its parts 66), kept when health is full. |
| The last nights' waits (2.59, 2.63.2) | §5: "hold the roof until dawn" | **The helicopter 60 game minutes** (120 before 2.59, 90 to 2.63.1), **the metro's gates 40** (90 before 2.58, 60 to 2.63.1). 2.59: at 120 the helicopter was the hardest last night for a bot. 2.63.2 (the user's request): 2.61's knockdown made the last nights with a bat harder; on 64 cities, a bat and three bandages, night 5, the helicopter 47 → 59 won and the metro 43 → 52 (night 1: 62 → 64, 52 → 59), about where they were before 2.61. |
| Clothes found (2.59) | §4.5 lists none | **Somebody's clothes lie in one piece of furniture of a house in the first district** (from the seed; never chained, never the story's furniture): found, the player wears them (`state.outfit`). To look at only: no rule reads them. |
| More kinds of the dead (2.60) | §4.2 names shamblers, runners and brutes | **Three more.** A crawler: by day in the dark indoors, from day 2 (one put down there in five): 18 health, sees three tiles, its bite (5) holds a leg two seconds. A screamer: nights from the second (one arrival in ten): 14 health; seeing the player it calls the dead within twelve tiles of them. A bloater: nights from the fourth (one in twelve): 44 health; put down, it bursts 1.7 tiles round (12 to the player, 16 to each of the dead there; since 2.61 a wall between stops it). The first day and night have shamblers only. |
| The unseen blow (2.60) | §4.3: "stealth is a choice" | **A blow on one of the dead that isn't after the player does double harm**: a bat knocks down a shambler standing in the dark in one (2.61). |
| Knocked down (2.61) | §4.2: "a knocked-down one stays down a while unless finished (a second blow to the downed, a blade, fire)" | **A blow without an edge that empties one leaves it lying 8 seconds** where it stood (not shoved), harmless and deaf and after nobody (the pack passes it, the HUD doesn't count it), then up after the player with a third of its health. Any blow, a bolt, fire or a burst finishes it; a blow or a bolt takes one on its feet first; the blow after a knockdown comes in 3 steps, not 12 (followed through). A knife, a cleaver or a spear puts it down for good; a crawler and a bloater are never only knocked down. Counted as put down when finished. |
| Bleeding (2.61) | §4.2: "a bite bleeds: health ticks down until bandaged" | **A bite adds half its harm to what is still to bleed** (16 at most), a point every two seconds, awake or asleep; **never the last point** (as food never kills, 2.58). A bandage or a medkit stops it at any health, pills don't; a fall leaves nothing bleeding. A window broken by bare hands bleeds 6. |
| Windows (2.61) | §4.3: "breaking a window … makes noise" | **Only a chained building's glass, from the street**: broken in 1.2 s with no tool, heard 14 tiles off (a crowbar's work 7); climbed through, the chain is lifted off from inside and the building is forced as by the crowbar. Boards and shutters can't be. Where windows are is one list for the rules and the picture (`content/windows.ts`). Since 2.64 the button takes a window faced only after the furniture and the cars beside: a search is never taken for breaking glass. |
| The others off the bus (2.61) | §5: notes tell "what happened to the other five from the bus" | **Four notes more** (fourteen), one for each passenger but Rook, in the park, the docks, the hospital quarter and the precinct, placed after everything else from a stream of their own (a saved city keeps its chains and loot). The reader feat is every note. |
| Breaking things (2.66) | §4.5 names the crowbar for "locked doors, crates" and nothing broken; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 1) | **Furniture, cars and wrecks break up, and an ordinary building's inner walls with a sledgehammer.** A blow every `BLOW.every` steps standing beside it, each heard (7 tiles on wood, 10 on metal, 12 a wall), costing a blow's energy and wearing what strikes: the best thing carried for the stuff (`content/salvage.ts`: fists and blades only wood; a bat both; an axe wood four times as fast as fists; a sledgehammer metal fastest; the crowbar both, unworn; tired, half). Begun by the blow's button with none of the dead in reach and a piece faced, or the open piece's Break; a wall only by the action button (a blow at the air never starts on one); walking off or a blow at the dead leaves the work. Done: furniture gone for good (its tiles walked on), what it held and what it is made of in a pile where it stood, the pile opened; a car or a wreck stripped (it stays, emptied; its metal at the player's feet; an alarmed one sounds); a wall a way through, the district put up again. Never: the story's furniture or a note's, a survivor's building, a station (workbench, generator), the landmarks' pieces, an outer wall, a landmark's or a survivor's walls. The **axe** (9, an edge) and the **sledgehammer** (11, knocks down) are weapons too, made at a bench; found only in a city begun from 2.66 (`state.rev` 2: garages, warehouses, the factory, the rangers', the boathouse). Kept by district in the save (`broken`, `stripped`, `holes`); a load ends work under way. |
| Building a base (2.67) | §4.4: a shelter is a building claimed, its doors barred; the testers' asks, 2026-11-20 (`phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md`, batch 2) | **With a hammer carried (a plank, scrap and tape, by hand; found from `state.rev` 3), eleven pieces go up on the tile faced** (`content/build.ts`), three seconds' hammering heard seven tiles off: a barricade (60), plank and metal walls (120, 250: they hide the player too), a door (100: the player walks through it, the dead don't), a chest (12 slots), a camp bed (slept in, home as any bed), a workbench (works as any), a campfire (a light of its own, `LIGHT_KIND.CAMP`: three tiles, six hours, four more a plank; it cooks), a table, a chair and a rug (walked over). Walls and doors stand across the way faced. **Never** in the tunnel, within two tiles of a checkpoint's street ends, beside a survivor or at a way out's tile (or the pier's end), on anything or anyone; 120 a district. **The dead after the player go round what's built; with no way round, they take the way through it and pound the first piece** (a point of its health every 10 steps, a brute's four; **since 2.68 the cheapest way, each piece weighing by the blows it takes**: a barricade gives before a plank wall, a plank wall before a metal one, and no step slips diagonally past a piece); one that isn't after anyone goes round. A piece's card: its health, Repair (whole again for half of what it took, for the share worn away: never dearer than a new one), Take down (half back, less as worn; a chest's things on the ground). **Comfort round a sleeper**: what's built within six tiles (a camp bed 3, a campfire burning 2, a chest, a table, a chair, a rug 1 each) and a light burning 1: a Hideout from 3 gives energy back asleep a quarter faster and health every 3 minutes, a Home from 6 half again faster and every 2. Barred street doors and shelters are as before.
