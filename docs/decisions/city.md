# Decisions: City Escape

The second mode (v2.46 on). Its design is [`../source/city_escape_design.md`](../source/city_escape_design.md);
a row here is where the build departs from it or settles what it leaves open, as it stands now.

| Topic (version) | The design says | The decision |
|---|---|---|
| A mode beside the prisons (2.46) | "a new mode accessible from the main menu … while preserving the original game mode" | **Its own simulation, no prison game in progress** while it runs (`play.side`), in `src/city/`, fetched when first opened. Nothing added to the prisons' lists or state; the title's button is the one visible change, and it is **hidden without `?city`** until stage C4 (the user's choice, 2026-11-13). |
| The city's size (2.46) | "several 64x64 maps with transition points" | **3 × 3 districts** (the user's choice), each its own 64 × 64 `World` built when entered; one is in memory at a time. A crossing is a checkpoint: stepping on a street's two border tiles puts the player a tile inside the neighbour, on the same street. |
| What a district is drawn from (2.46) | §2: blocks, buildings, landmarks | **The prisons' own tiles and furniture**: walls, floors, doors and props as the legend has them (`rows` in the map's characters), so the view draws them with no new tile kind; streets are `ROAD`, pavements and car parks `ASPHALT` (an indoor kind would be roofed). What stands on the streets is the city's own (`DECO`), solid through `World.parked`. Street doors are yellow: open to all. |
| Lanes (2.46) | §2: streets meet at the border | **Three lanes each way per grid column and row**, at 11, 30 and 49 tiles ±2, two tiles wide; a district has every lane a checkpoint uses and three in four of the others, two at least each way. |
| The clock (2.46) | §3: a day is 24 real minutes | The prisons' pace (20 steps a game minute), from **06:30 on day 1**. Night is the prisons' (21:00 to 05:00, `NIGHT_FROM`/`NIGHT_TO`). |
| The map's fog (2.46) | §2: districts fill in as they are walked | **A disc of 7 tiles** round the player, through walls (a map of where they have been, not of what they saw); a bit a tile per district in the save. |
| Saving (2.46) | not said | **One city in progress**, in its own slot (`pe3d.city.save`), written on entering a district, on pausing and when the page is hidden or closed; Reset progress wipes it. No run log or replay. The Intake is asked before a first city, as before a first prison. |
| Death's two modes (2.46) | §4.6 | **Chosen on the new city's card and stored** (`MODE`). Since 2.47 hunger and the dead kill: a Survivor wakes at home (the row below), Hardcore loses the city and its save. |
| Items (2.47) | §4.5: "the city's own items and recipes kept apart from the prisons' lists" | **A list of its own** (`CITY_ITEMS`, append only), each with a shape from the item models and its own colours; its pockets are its own (`Stack`: an item and a count), not the prisons' `ItemState`: simpler, and nothing of a prison's contraband, uses or slots applies. The icons are taken by the prisons' studio (`render/itemIcons.ts`: `photos`). |
| Searching (2.47) | §3: "search homes, shops, cars and bins" | **Furniture only** for now (cars and the street's wrecks with C2b). A piece's contents are drawn when first opened, from the seed, the district and the piece's number: the same in every play of a city, and nothing is stored for what was never opened. |
| Sleep and home (2.47) | §4.4: shelters claimed, one of them home | Until shelters (C2c), **any searched bed can be slept in, and the last one is home**. Sleep is refused by day unless tired (energy under 60), and while starving. |
| Hunger's pace (2.47) | §4.1: "falls all day" | A full bar lasts **about 30 hours awake** (55 thousandths a minute), half that pace asleep; a scavenger bot eats about three things a day and finds about four. Running doesn't cost more yet (there's no run). |
| A Survivor's fall (2.47) | §4.6: "the night's horde has grown by a night" | Wakes at home at **06:30 the next morning** with 40 health, at least 15 hunger (a fall is no meal: the review's exploit) and 60 energy; **each fall counts as a night more** for the night's dead. |
| The dead, one district at a time (2.47) | §4.2: "at night they spawn at the dark places and roam" | **Only the district the player is in has any**, put down afresh at each entry from the hour, the day and the district's danger: a checkpoint is a way out of a chase, and nothing is simulated or saved for the other eight. Runners and brutes come with stage C3. |
| A bite's wind-up (2.47) | §4.2: "a grab, then bites while held: breaking free costs energy" | No grab: **a lunge of nine steps before each first bite, begun again when struck**. Measured on the bot: without it one shambler cost two bites to put down with fists and a week cost 25 falls; with it, none and 1.7. |
| Bodies (2.47) | not said | **Nobody walks through the dead**: the player's step into one isn't taken (a doorway is held, and a room can be shut in on them), nor one of a chasing pack's into another of it; the dead wandering pass each other (two face to face in an alley would stand there for good). |
| The blow (2.47) | §4.5: weapons "wearing out" | **The best weapon in the pockets strikes, with no wear yet** (wear comes with crafting, C2c). Fists 6, a bat 18, a knife 22 against a shambler's 30. |
| Sleeping before shelters (2.47) | §4.4 | A sleeper is seen only from two tiles, and a bite wakes them. Nights are meant to be broken until doors can be barred (C2c). |
| Recipes (2.47; three taught since 2.48) | §4.5: weapons "crafted and wearing out"; cooking on a fire | **Eight recipes, all known from the start** (finding the parts is the game): three by hand, three at a workbench, two with a camp stove carried (a fire barrel and light wait for a later slice). The crossbow, the molotov and the noise lure wait for a way to aim (stage C3). |
| Wear (2.47) | §4.5 | **A weapon's count in its pocket is its blows left**, so a weapon never stacks and a save needs no new field; it wears only when a blow lands. **One weapon strikes**: the strongest carried (of two as strong, the longer), with its own reach; there's no choosing yet. |
| Shelters (2.47) | §4.4: "any building with a bed can be claimed once cleared: bar its doors, light it, stash loot in it" | **A building is a shelter when every street door is barred** with boards (a recipe); no bed is needed and nothing checks it is cleared: one of the dead shut in with the player is the player's problem. The player passes their own barred door; shamblers never break one (brutes will, C3). |
| The kit (2.48) | §4.5: "parts for the ways out, one or two per district" among the items | **The story's things are not items**: a kit of their own (a bit each in the state), never in the eight pockets, never dropped, kept through a Survivor's fall. Each lies in one piece of furniture chosen from the seed and is found on its first search, asked for yet or not. |
| The radio (2.48) | §5: "a hand-cranked radio in the first shelter"; act 2, "crank the radio; fetch what Dispatch asks (batteries, an aerial)" | In the **nearest house to the start with a bed**, in its furniture. It is heard as soon as found; it **mends itself once the aerial and two batteries are in hand** (the batteries leave the pockets, said by a toast): no cranking, no step to forget. |
| Scouting and choosing (2.48) | §5, act 3: "Dispatch names three ways out; scout each" | A way out is scouted by **walking into its landmark**, counted whenever it happens; **one scouted is enough to choose**, in the journal, and another may be chosen until one is set going. The things found for a way not taken stay in the kit. |
| What the ways take (2.48) | §5: fuel and "a torch that lasts" among the parts | **Three kit things each**, none from the pockets: the boat's fuel is a drum at the filling station, the metro's light a ranger's lamp (a found torch is rare, and pocket fuel is spent on the stove). The flare gun and the gate key card lie in the police armoury, **locked until the fence's key** (below). |
| The helicopter's night (2.48) | §5: "hold the pad till dawn" | The beacon is lit **after dark only** and the helicopter comes **120 game minutes later** to whoever stands in the hospital (the roof isn't a floor of its own). Dawn isn't waited for: lit at 21:00 or at 04:00, it is the same two hours. Barred doors are torn open one every 20 minutes, **anywhere in the district** (the building the player is in first): no boarded house sits the night out. |
| The metro's night (2.48) | §5: "the darkest place in the city, on foot" | **The tunnel itself isn't walked** (it would be a tenth map). The last night is the wait in the station: the generator's 90 minutes, the dead coming up inside it and boards torn off as for the beacon; then the button takes the tunnel. |
| The boat's night (2.48) | §5: "get to the pier through the fog; quiet beats strong" | The engine is fitted at any hour and stays fitted; the boat leaves from **the pier's end between 23:00 and 02:00**. With the engine in, the docks' nights are in fog: the dead see two tiles and hear twice as far, eight more of them. |
| Failing a last night (2.48) | not said | Leaving the district or falling **stops the beacon or the generator and loses nothing**: the kit and the choice are kept, another night will do. A Survivor's falls still count as nights more for the dead. |
| Survivors (2.48) | §5: "survivors hold a shelter in three or four districts … each gives two or three errands for recipes, trade and a line of the story. One of them is another prisoner from the bus"; §4.4: "safe by nature" | **Four, standing still** on a tile of a building of theirs (the fence in the mall, the nurse in a clinic, the mechanic in a garage, the ranger in the lodge), **two errands each**. Safe: nobody harms them, and none of the dead are put down in their building (the dead may still follow the player in). The fence is the one from the bus. |
| Errands (2.48) | not said how they are taken or paid | **The action meets them** (the first errand asked) and is a word after; **their card's button hands the errand over** when its things are in the pockets or enough of the dead were put down since it was asked (a word never takes anything: the radio's batteries are safe). No accept or refuse. Three first errands teach a recipe (medkit, cleaver, trail ration). |
| The armoury's key (2.48) | §4.5: "the police station the armoury (a locked door: its key is a quest)" | No door: **the two story things in the police station are locked** until the fence's first errand (three bits of cloth) pays the key. The helicopter and the metro need it; the boat doesn't. |
| Swaps (2.48) | §5: "trade" | **Three fixed swaps a survivor**, as often as wanted, only beside them; no money and no stock. |
| Runners and brutes (2.48) | §4.2: runners from night 3, "as fast as the player, rarer, weaker"; brutes from night 5, "one per hard district … break barricades" | Runners **one arrival in eight, a little slower than the player** (168 against 184: caught only when tired, hungry or cornered); **one brute a district a night** where the danger is 2 or more, **pounding a barred door open in 80 steps** (it goes for a door only after someone it has seen: a sleeper behind boards isn't looked for). At night only, in the streets; no draw before the third night. |
| The city's records (2.48) | §7: "the prisons' achievements and stats (the city gets its own)" | **Ten feats and the ways out taken**, in a slot of their own, counted when a city is left and not before; shown on the end card and from the city's menu. The prisons' achievements never see them. |
| How to play and a tutorial (2.49) | roadmap C4: "How to play's topics and pictures, a short tutorial" | **A tab of its own** in How to play (nine topics), and **seven one-time tips** in place of a lesson: the city has no scripted day to teach on, and the first act's list already leads. |
| The finish's look and sound (2.49) | roadmap C4: "the palette and dressing (overgrowth, dust, signage, the landmarks' own models), sounds and music of its own" | **Awnings by use, weeds, litter, the survivors' own models; the dead's and the radio's own sounds.** Since 2.50 **the landmarks have their own models**, render only and nowhere anyone walks (a coping, a pictogram sign without words, corner pieces; the helipad, the beacon and the helicopter, the boat at the pier, the grille over the metro's stairs). Since 2.51 **a score of its own**: E Phrygian, five moods (day, night, shelter, chase, the last stand) picked from what is happening (the last stand's only where the dead are called: the boat's, while its tide is in); the prisons' tension theme no longer plays there. |
| Shown to everyone (2.49) | the user's choice: "hidden until complete" | The four stages built, **the entry is on the title for everyone from 2.49.0**; what only a person can check (touch, a real pad, a real phone) is listed in `BACKLOG.md` §2. |
| Light and fire (2.52) | §4.3: "a lit tile is one within a light's reach (a fire barrel 4 tiles, a lamp 3, a generator's lamps a whole room). The dead will not stand in strong light unless chasing"; §4.1: light "a state the HUD shows: lit, dim or dark" | **As written, lit from the pockets**: fuel used beside a street barrel (4 tiles, 8 h; it cooks) or a generator (its building, 12 h), a lantern made and set down (3 tiles, 10 h), the torch a dim light on a battery (4 h; seen at 6 tiles behind it, not 4). Claude's picks: light stops at walls; **a brute walks through it**, and so do the dead the beacon and the metro's generator call; none is put down in it; a sleeper in it is seen at the night's four tiles (a lantern is no barred door); the dead beyond the player's light at night are drawn as shapes and eyes. Dark interiors by day and fire as a weapon are not built. |
