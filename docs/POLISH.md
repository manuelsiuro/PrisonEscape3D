# Gameplay polish: what's been done

One row per area of the game. Read an area's row before changing it, and update it when the work
is released (the `polish-gameplay` skill does both). The detail is in `HISTORY.md`, the rules in
`PLAN.md` §2. `polishtest` (npm test) checks this table's shape and that each version is in the history.

**State:** *Done*: reworked or reviewed at the user's request and checked in the browser (which doesn't
mean finished). *Partly*: one side of it was reworked, the rest wasn't looked at. *Not reviewed*: built
and tested when it shipped, but no polish pass, so nothing is known either way.

| Area | Version | State | The user's choices | Known limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The library and Intellect | 2.31.0 | Done | Reading is a game of its own: a page on screen, a marker along it, one button to underline the key words as it reaches them; a slow reader still finishes, tapping away gains nothing, about 5 s a set kept up with; still +2 for 10 energy. A daily limit: 6 sets, then "your head's full" till morning; kiosk courses, checkers and quiz night teach on top. Subjects (tools, locks and fakes, weapons, everyday things): each mark's recipe comes from the one being read, and the panel shows what's next. Inmates choose books and read at the tables, every library has chairs, the player reads sitting (at the shelf with none free), and a librarian at a table suggests a subject and says when the head is full. Claude's picks: the bar fills by itself in about 20 s; a stray press spends the next key word; 6 key words a page, 3 pages a book; book in hand only in the library, put back on leaving; a chair gives no extra energy while reading, so a set costs what the card says; a chair in reach comes before anyone to talk to, with a book in hand; a blow ends the reading; the librarian's chair is at the end of a table, the inmate an ordinary one who also keeps their job in the Work Period; Irongate gets 3 chairs, San Pancho stays at 2 (no room). Two older problems fixed with it at the user's word: San Pancho's ambushes (none before 12:00 on day 1, one a prison day, not on a Bad Blood Daily, a challenge taken up); nobody jumps the player on the morning of a parole hearing or a release, and whoever was after them lets it go | Hands off, a set nets about no energy: 20 s of standing about gives the 10 back, as a slow set at the gym does. Reading on a chair costs what it costs at the shelf, so sitting is a matter of the arrows and the look, not of a reward. Book text is written for this and short. A player who does nothing at San Pancho is still floored on day 1, from 15:00 on, by a grudge they could have fought: a lawless prison stays one. Not played by hand on a real phone (touch simulated, timing on a real screen unmeasured); sounds not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
| Jobs and the Work Period | 2.30.0 | Done | Same quotas and timings, a short daily chore made clear and alive: arrows to the next station, a line in the HUD saying what's next, machines that run. Inmates really do their jobs: from station to station carrying the work, machines running for them, the janitor mopping the corridors, the mailman walking to people. A warning while the quota can still be met; being fired stays as it was. Guards mind strangers: seen in a work room that isn't yours in the Work Period, told to leave, a little heat if you stay, never a chase. Claude's picks: the warning at 13:45 (the mail round 13:00, if it isn't begun), since from nothing 14:30 left too little time in a third of the jobs, with a last call at 14:30; 5 s to leave, then +5% and 1% a second, stopping at 30%; a janitor or mailman goes anywhere; the garden doesn't count; a pumpkin to carve, the fixer's mug and a word with a worker are let be; what's taken goes into the hand; the mop works from the pockets; none of it at the Training Block | Some rooms' bins stand against the south wall, half hidden by it, so the marker and arrows do that work. Merchants and others who hold a job now move about at work, as any inmate does in free time. Being told to leave isn't counted as being spotted; staying is. Not played by hand on a real phone (touch simulated); sounds not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
| Exercise and training | 2.29.0 | Done | A set is pressed in time with a beat, each machine at its own pace: on the beat counts far more, mashing gains nothing, a slow hand still finishes; still +2 for 12 energy. Inmates use the machines in the exercise hour and free time and give way: "Ask for a turn", about 2 s, and it's yours, never refused. The punching bag (Speed) and mat (Strength) work, and every gym has both. Claude's picks: the beats and how much a press counts; the bar no longer drains; on the bench lying down with a real bar; two inmates in three use machines, never a merchant or the fixer; none at the Training Block, whose lessons ask for things by name; someone on a machine is asked rather than talked to, and with no use for it (too tired, at 100) the button talks to them instead | Strength and Speed still reach 100 quickly (+20 in one exercise hour): the design document's numbers, left alone (Intellect has had a daily limit since 2.31). You can walk through a bag, as you could when it was decor, to keep old runs exact. Not played by hand on a real phone (touch simulated; press timing on a real screen unmeasured); sounds not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
| Lights Out and sleep | 2.28.0 | Done | At night energy comes back only asleep; a few hours fill it, a whole night out means starting the day on what's left. The bed's button adapts at night: dummy in hand Tuck in, dummy in bed Take out, then Sleep; other things in the bed wait for morning. At a bed check, in your cell is enough, and the guard says something if you're up, at no cost. Claude's picks: awake, energy still creeps back to 20% so nobody is stranded; asleep a point every 2 minutes; 25 minutes to get home instead of 15, a warning at 22:50 and no searchlights on the walk; the cell block's lights go out at 23:25; the dummy drawn in the bed; a morning toast | San Pancho's tunnel bot, out of energy underground, gets out at 00:04 instead of 23:24. Stalag's walk home from the sports ground, shut by then, takes 24.7 of the 25 minutes. Not played by hand on a real phone; the lights' sound not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
| Roll call | 2.27.0 | Done | Counted on your own orange square only (off it to the end: +25% heat, no lockdown; out of the yard: a lockdown, as ever); the warden walks the line and calls you first, the button says "Here!" and you press it. Claude's picks: +5% heat for a call unanswered in 5 s, short of a chase; the 2.23 half hour stays; the shakedown stays at 08:00, shouted and kept on the pill through roll call. On screen it's "a guard" and their name, since the game's Warden is someone else | Not played by hand on a real phone; sounds not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
| Showers | 2.26.0 | Done | The action button turns a shower on and walking away turns it off; inmates take turns, wait aside and dry off (maps unchanged); shorts and a towel while washing and drying, looks only; an ambush warns first, everywhere (1.5 s, the length was Claude's pick). Claude's picks too, no objection: heat under 25% falls a point faster every 2 s while washing; being in the room still counts | Since 2.31 San Pancho deals one ambush a day and none in the first morning, so the warning means something: over 12 shower blocks on day 1 a bot that stands there is knocked out 10 times, one that leaves 6, one that hits back 4. Not played by hand on a real phone; sounds not heard; French not read by a native speaker |
