# History: v2.20 to v2.29

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser, and what was accepted as it is. Split from `docs/HISTORY.md` by version on 2026-11-06, word for word; `HISTORY.md` is the index.

- **P41 The manhunt (v2.20.0)**, the roadmap's third phase (§13, built by an agent alongside P39, P40 and P42): over the wall is no longer the end.
