# History: v2.55 to v2.59

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **City Escape: weapons thrown or shot (v2.55.0)**, the user's request (2026-11-14:
  "update the documentation then continue to the next step", the next of `BACKLOG.md` §4b's parked
  items); the design's §4.5 and §4.3.
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (a row of its own): thrown from the pockets
    with Use, shot with the blow's own button, no aiming but the way faced; fire is no light, the dead
    don't avoid it, and it is gone when the district is left.
  - **Built:** `content/ranged.ts`, `sim/ranged.ts`. An **empty bottle** thrown: six tiles the way
    faced or onto the nearest of the dead ahead, heard eight tiles round. A **molotov** (a new item; a
    bottle, fuel, tape, by hand): the tile it breaks on and the open ones round it burn 240 steps
    (`state.fires`, the one new field, optional on load, made sound before the city is built), 8 health
    every 10 steps from each of the dead standing in it, 4 from the player. The **crossbow** and its
    **bolts** (new items, made at a bench): `strike` shoots the nearest of the dead ahead that the blow
    wouldn't reach, within seven tiles (26 a bolt, every 30 steps, heard two tiles off, no energy spent,
    40 shots, one bolt in two left where it struck). Fifteen recipes. On screen: the pockets' Throw, the
    bottle's arc and the bolt's streak, flames on a scorched tile, a red flash and a toast in the fire,
    `Sound.shatter` and `Sound.twang`, a tip, three lines in `cityFight`'s help.
  - **Old cities:** the bots throw nothing and carry no crossbow: their runs are the same step for step
    (four snapshots moved by the state's fingerprint, `citymaketest` by its recipes' line).
  - **An independent review** (the diff; two probes), seven findings, all fixed: fires stood still
    while the player slept (free to sleep in, flames all night); a bottle or a molotov flew through a
    barred door and burnt what stood behind the boards; any of the dead in reach, even behind the
    player or through a wall, took the shot away for a blow in the air; one molotov's crash called the
    whole street into its fire (14 of 20 put down in the probe, brutes among them: its noise is four
    tiles now, not eight); the test's "hears nothing" stood beyond a blow's hearing too, and sleep, a
    barred door and a fall weren't tested; the break was heard before the arc landed; being burnt showed
    nothing.
  - **Accepted:** a brute that stands in a fire its whole time falls to it; fire burns round a survivor
    and harms nobody there; a diagonal throw may clip a building's corner.
  - **Not checked:** touch, a real pad, a real phone; the three sizes (seen at 1040 × 480); the fire at
    night; the new sounds by ear; French on screen; the bottle's arc and the bolt's streak were not
    caught in a picture (a quarter of a second each).
  - **Found in the browser:** nothing to change: a molotov thrown and the crossbow shot with the real
    buttons, before the review's fixes.
- **City Escape: the metro's tunnel, walked (v2.56.0, not released yet)**, the user's request
  (2026-11-14: "continue", the last of `BACKLOG.md` §4b's parked items); the design's §5 ("the darkest
  place in the city, on foot").
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (the metro's night): a tenth map entered at the
    open gates, one way to a ladder, the ranger's lamp its light, eight of the dead, no way back up.
  - **Built:** `gen/tunnel.ts` (`TUNNEL` 9, `PLACES` 10): a way three tiles wide from x = 8 to x = 55
    with turns, rooms off it (a crate or a bin), things along its sides. `City.descend` from `storyAct`;
    `storyMoved` wins at the ladder's foot; `lampLit` (a dim light, seen at six tiles); the tunnel's own
    `quota`; no dusk, night or dawn down there. A tenth map seen in saves (nine load). On screen: its
    name and "Under the wall", midnight's dark at any hour, rails and sleepers, the ladder under a shaft
    of light, the lamp's pill, the journal's line, the hold's music; the city's map shows the player at
    the metro's stairs. The help's ways page says it.
  - **Measured:** 60 tunnels, 82 tiles long on average (54 to 107), eight turns; with the dead about a
    bot with a bat and three bandages gets through on 12 cities of 12; `citystorytest`'s last nights,
    the metro 4 of 6 (5 before the tunnel: one more bot's bat was worn out by the wait). Twelve of the
    dead was 3 of 6.
  - **Moved snapshots,** read: `citytest` (a tenth map in a save's fingerprint), `citystorytest` (the
    generator's line, the metro bots' fingerprints with the same days, the last nights' count).
  - **An independent review** (the diff; four probes, the generator on 1,500 seeds), seven findings,
    all fixed: a save put in the tunnel with another way out, or none, won that way at the ladder (such
    a save loads at the metro's stairs); a fall left everything carried behind another night of the
    generator, fought without it (it lies at the top of the stairs); the surface's dusk, nightfall and
    dawn were said underground, the HUD showed a sun, and at 21:00 the tunnel's dead began to wander;
    the next district's rooftops were drawn beyond the tunnel's rock; things on opposite sides of two
    tiles running left one tile to pass (never on the tile after one that has something); the test's
    save was made with no dead about, its "eight" was "more than none", a constant was tested against
    itself; a comment out of place, the save's name frozen in one language.
  - **Accepted:** bare-handed the tunnel is nearly impassable (the review's bot: 1 of 12); a tunnel in
    thirty has no room to search; the escape is counted a tile before the ladder's own.
  - **Not checked:** touch, a real pad, a real phone; the three sizes (seen at 1040 × 480); the descent's
    blink and banner, the ladder and the end card on screen; the music down there by ear; French on
    screen; `perfprobe` on a map that is mostly rock.
  - **Found in the browser:** the lamp's pool fell off the map's west edge at the stairs (both ends
    moved eight tiles in).
- **City Escape: the light's line follows the language (v2.56.1, not released yet)**, found in the
  passes after 2.56 (2026-11-14: the "not checked" of the last releases looked at in the browser).

  - **Looked at meanwhile, nothing to change:** the tunnel taken by the real button, its HUD at

    the journal's line, the lamp, the molotov's card).
  - **Not checked:** no test draws the HUD; the rest of 2.50 to 2.56's lists stands (touch, a real
