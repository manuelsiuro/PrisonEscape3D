# History: v2.55 to v2.59

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **City Escape: weapons thrown or shot (v2.55.0, not released yet)**, the user's request (2026-11-14:
  "update the documentation then continue to the next step", the next of `BACKLOG.md` §4b's parked
  items); the design's §4.5 and §4.3.
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (a row of its own): thrown from the pockets
    with Use, shot with the blow's own button, no aiming but the way faced; fire is no light, the dead
    don't avoid it, and it is gone when the district is left.
  - **Built:** `content/ranged.ts`, `sim/ranged.ts`. An **empty bottle** thrown: six tiles the way
    faced or onto the nearest of the dead ahead, heard eight tiles round. A **molotov** (a new item; a
    bottle, fuel, tape, by hand): the tile it breaks on and the open ones round it burn 240 steps
    (`state.fires`, the one new field, optional on load, made sound before the city is built), 8 health
    every 10 steps from each of the dead standing in it, 4 from the player. The **crossbow** and its
    **bolts** (new items, made at a bench): `strike` shoots the nearest of the dead ahead that the blow
    wouldn't reach, within seven tiles (26 a bolt, every 30 steps, heard two tiles off, no energy spent,
    40 shots, one bolt in two left where it struck). Fifteen recipes. On screen: the pockets' Throw, the
    bottle's arc and the bolt's streak, flames on a scorched tile, a red flash and a toast in the fire,
    `Sound.shatter` and `Sound.twang`, a tip, three lines in `cityFight`'s help.
  - **Old cities:** the bots throw nothing and carry no crossbow: their runs are the same step for step
    (four snapshots moved by the state's fingerprint, `citymaketest` by its recipes' line).
  - **An independent review** (the diff; two probes), seven findings, all fixed: fires stood still
    while the player slept (free to sleep in, flames all night); a bottle or a molotov flew through a
    barred door and burnt what stood behind the boards; any of the dead in reach, even behind the
    player or through a wall, took the shot away for a blow in the air; one molotov's crash called the
    whole street into its fire (14 of 20 put down in the probe, brutes among them: its noise is four
    tiles now, not eight); the test's "hears nothing" stood beyond a blow's hearing too, and sleep, a
    barred door and a fall weren't tested; the break was heard before the arc landed; being burnt showed
    nothing.
  - **Accepted:** a brute that stands in a fire its whole time falls to it; fire burns round a survivor
    and harms nobody there; a diagonal throw may clip a building's corner.
  - **Not checked:** touch, a real pad, a real phone; the three sizes (seen at 1040 × 480); the fire at
    night; the new sounds by ear; French on screen; the bottle's arc and the bolt's streak were not
    caught in a picture (a quarter of a second each).
  - **Found in the browser:** nothing to change: a molotov thrown and the crossbow shot with the real
    buttons, before the review's fixes.
