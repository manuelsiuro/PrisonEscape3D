# History: v2.70 to v2.74

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **City Escape, survival with weight (v2.70.0, not released yet)**, the fifth batch of stage C5, Make it yours (the
  user's go, 2026-11-20: "start batch 5").
  - **Choices:** `phases/11-city-make-it-yours.md` (batch 5, "As built") and `decisions/city-c5.md`'s rows (Thirst and the
    states, Weather, Water, the garden and fishing; Recipes and A Survivor's fall amended). Claude's picks are in those
    rows: the numbers, everything in a city begun from 2.70 only, hens left for batch 6, fishing a timed cast.
  - **Built:** `content/survival.ts` and `sim/survival.ts` (thirst, the states, food going off at dawn, the weather from
    the seed, water drunk and fished, a planter's garden and a rain barrel worked out from the days' rain, read without
    writing for the view, a smoker); `CITY_REV` 6; the items (`use.thirst`, `mood`, `settles`, `stanch`, `dry`) and
    twelve new ones, eight recipes (`smoker`), three pieces (`manual`, `grows`), three manuals, revision 6's loot (water
    where it'd be kept, dirty water in toilets, seeds, herbs, coffee, the raincoat, the manuals); the dead's hearing in
    the rain and sight in the fog; the bots drink; the save's `thirst` and `moods`. The screens: the thirst meter, the
    states' chips with their time left (wrapping short of the corner map on a phone), the weather beside the day, the

  - **Found:** writing the test: a player with no water kept healing (no health back at none now). Running the suite:
    a fall from thirst woke the player still parched, to fall again before noon (a little thirst on waking, as hunger).
    Measuring: the scavenger bot fell to thirst ten times in twenty weeks, parched a tenth of the time, and the story bot
    with the dead away was parched a quarter of its time and days slower (water put where it'd be kept, thirst a day
    long rather than twenty hours, a bottle 50 rather than 40); a fresh thing gone off altogether left its slot holding
    none of it, which filled a bot's pockets till it dropped the cloth an errand wanted, and stuck it in act 4 (the slot
    freed now). The numbers that stand are in `decisions/city-c5.md`'s Thirst row. In the browser: the rain gone after
    a district was put up again (put back); one-pixel streaks too faint (brighter, more); four meters and the states' chips under the corner map on a 412-wide phone
    (shorter bars, the chips wrapping). By the independent review: thirst never woke a sleeper, and a parched player
    could go to bed and fall asleep for good (woken now, and refused); a cast went on with the rod put in the bag,
    free casts that never wore it (over now); a city begun before 2.70 listed a planter, a barrel and recipes nothing
    there could make work (hidden there); two minutes in bed before six made a player rested for twelve hours (six
    hours' sleep now); the barrel's water drawn stale after a drink; a rod in the pockets made a parched player fish
    (a drink first under half); the water took a chained window's button by the sea (after it now); "not enough to
    build that" for a planter without seeds (its own lines); the minute's new stop changed a city under way at a fall
    (only in a new city); a single fresh thing never going off (half rounded up); the rain falling indoors and the
    tunnel saying "clear" at dawn. All fixed, the review's cases in `cityneedstest`.
