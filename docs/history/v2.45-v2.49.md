# History: v2.45 to v2.49

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **Gamepad support (v2.45.0)**, the user's request (2026-11-13: "start the gamepad support",
  session 10 of the improvement queue, parked in `BACKLOG.md` §3 since 2026-11-06).
  - **Choices:** the whole game by pad, menus included; texts naming the pad's buttons while it's in hand (Xbox,
    PlayStation or Nintendo labels, by the pad's id), back to keys on a key or a touch; a fixed layout (Xbox names: A acts,
    X the tool held, Y locks on, LT blocks held, RT throws, held it aims with the right stick, LB and RB along the hotbar,
    L3 drops, R3 digs up held, the right stick zooms, View a wheel of the panels, Menu pauses, B closes); rumble where a
    phone vibrates, under the same setting. All four were the recommended options.
  - **Built:** `input/pad.ts` (the standard mapping read each frame, one pad followed, the last touched; a button held
    as a pad is found or the page comes back ignored until let go; the sticks' dead zone; a stick as an arrow with give;
    `nextBox`, the D-pad's choice of the next button on a card); `play/pad.ts` (the pad sends the bound key of each
    action as synthetic key events, so every card and minigame works as it does by keyboard; on a card or a screen the
    D-pad moves the focus and A clicks it; cards played with the arrows, from the sim's state, get the arrows instead;
    a card just opened gets its first choice, one redrawn gets its choice back; a way out, ×, Stop or Give up, is never
    chosen for the player, so A can't give up a tattoo; the right stick scrolls a card or screen, else zooms; the
    pockets from the wheel, the hotbar and an item's menu chosen with the D-pad); `ui/padWheel.ts`; the pad's aim
    (`padAimStart`, `padAimMove`, `padAimThrow`); `keys.ts`'s Escape chain as `closeSomething` (B closes, never
    pauses); `Controls.padStick` (16 ways); the rumble through `haptics.ts` (`setRumble`, the setting shown when a pad
    rumbles); sound started by a pad's press where the browser counts it, a pad's synthetic keys no longer using up
    the gesture that starts it; `t()` choosing a text's `…Pad` twin while a pad is in hand (59 twins: lesson steps,
    hints and card notes that name the mouse, the digits, Esc, X or L), `%Escape%` and `%PadRT%`-style placeholders;
    How to play's Gamepad topic and the Controls tab's Gamepad list; the focus ring with the pad in hand
    (`45-pad.css`); `padtest`, `scripts/dev/fakepad.ts`; `i18ntest` and `keystest` read the twins.
  - **Found while building:** the intake card isn't a `.modal` (it is a screen now); `help.k`'s keyboard list would
    have named pad buttons (it names keys always); a pad's first press as "Xbox Wireless Controller" read as Sony's
    "Wireless Controller"; at the gym, a slanted stick stepped off the bench (the stick is the arrows there, as the
    keys are); the tattoo's needle wants the stick's 16 ways, not arrows; a long talk panel couldn't be read past its
    disabled rows (the right stick scrolls it); an aim armed from the item's menu said "Click where to throw".
  - **Found by the review, fixed:** the hoop couldn't be shot (a pad's Space went to the court's focused pad, which
    keys.ts leaves to the browser; a real key's only now); the stick, walking, also moved a card's choice (on the
    tattoo's card onto Give up: only the D-pad chooses on a card); the lesson card kept the old words when the pad was
    picked up or put down (its cache key has `textStamp()`); five hints and notes still named the mouse or the digits
    (twins); ~30 places chose the touch words before `t()` (`touchWords()`: a pad in hand on a touch screen gets the
    pad's); RT held in the manhunt aimed at nothing (a stone straight ahead there); a button held as a key was pressed
    swallowed the next press (let go when the pad's put down); a pad on the desk rumbled for a keyboard player. Also:
    a generic "Wireless Controller" took PlayStation labels; a drifting second pad could take over; the wheel stayed
    under a screen; B shut an item's menu that crafting hid; the manhunt had no help by pad (its wheel: How to play).
  - **After the review, the user's request** (2026-11-13: "My bluetooth gamepad is a 8bitdo sf 30 pro, can you use A, B,
    X, Y button instead of current XBox button for help in game, or maybe add a settings to select gamepad type"): 8BitDo
    pads read by name or vendor (2dc8) get their Super Nintendo letters (B at the bottom, A on the right; L2, R2, Select,
    Start), and Settings, Gamepad buttons (shown with a pad plugged in) picks the letters: Auto, Xbox, PlayStation,
    Nintendo, 8BitDo (`Settings.padButtons`, `setPadPick`), for a pad that passes for another (an 8BitDo in X-input mode
    says it's an Xbox pad, in Switch mode a Pro Controller). The buttons' places, so what each does, stay as they were.
  - **Played in the browser** with the pretend pad: the title, the intake, the first two lessons and the penalties
    lesson from the Tutorial menu (a shootout won by □ and the D-pad on a PlayStation pad's labels), the gym, a talk
    panel, the pockets and an item's menu, all three throws, the pause, settings and its sliders, How to play's tabs,
    the wheel at desktop, 412×915 and 915×412 sizes; the hoop, after the review; the console clean. Every lesson
    step's words, the pad's included, fit the card at 697×274 in both languages (834).
  - **Not checked:** a real pad (none here: Chrome, Firefox and Safari map some pads differently, and a stick's feel
    and the rumble are only felt), every card in every minigame by pad (most were reached through the generic rules,
    not each played), a pad on a phone or a tablet.
