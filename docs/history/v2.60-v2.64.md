# History: v2.60 to v2.64

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **City Escape: the fight with the dead gone over, three more kinds of them (v2.60.0)**,
  the user's request (2026-11-15: "fight with zombie should be polish too including UI and 3D, and more
  differents zombies style and variety").
  - **Choices (Claude's, told to the user; the numbers in `content/dead.ts`):** variety is both looks
    and rules. Three kinds more, each asking something else of the player: a crawler (the dark indoors
    by day, from day 2: look before walking in), a screamer (nights, from the second: put it down first,
    or run), a bloater (nights, from the fourth: keep a spear's length, a bolt or fire). A blow on one
    that isn't after anyone does double: the dark rooms' dead can be come up behind. The first day and
    night are as they were. No new button: the fight stays one press.
  - **Built, the rules:** `ZKIND` 3 to 5, `kindStats`, `putDown` (every death: said as `fell`; a
    bloater's burst, the dead it fells put down in their turn), `state.snare` (a crawler's hold),
    `BLOW.unseen`; `swing` says `unseen` and the health `left`.
  - **Built, the picture (`render/horde.ts`):** eleven looks (eight worn by district: civilians,
    office, scrubs, workers, police, soldiers, hoodies, the bus's orange only where the city starts;
    a brute's, a screamer's, a bloater's their own), legs that stride, arms that hang, reach, claw, are
    flung up by a blow, a runner low with its arms back, a crawler flat on its front; a flush where a
    blow lands; its health over the head of one struck; a body that topples and sinks where one is put
    down. A dozen draws whatever their number (it was one).
  - **Built, the fight on screen:** the player's arm swings and the weapon that strikes is in the
    hand; a burst and a jolt where a blow lands, harder for one put down or caught unawares; the
    player flinches at a bite; the Hit button shows what strikes, the blows left round it and by
    number (red at five), greyed while the arm comes back; the screen's edges pulse at 25 health and
    under. Sounds of their own: a blow landing, a screamer, a bloater's breath and its burst, a
    crawler. Toasts and a tip for each kind and for the unseen blow; the dead's and the fight's pages
    brought up to date, four pictures retaken.
  - **Measured:** 12 cities, every district put down three times: day 3, 15% crawlers; night 2, 8%
    screamers; night 6, 79% shamblers, 10% runners, 1% brutes, 6% screamers, 5% bloaters. The
    scavenger falls 2.3 times a week as before (44 put down, 65 bites, from 41 and 61).
  - **Moved snapshots:** `citytest`, `cityzedtest` (the state's fingerprints: `snare`), `citylighttest`
    (the day's dead: a draw more each), `citysurvivetest`, `citystorytest`, `citytooltest` (runs moved
    by the new draws from the second night on), `i18ntest`, `helptest`, `polishtest`.
  - **Found by the independent review, fixed:** in Hardcore a bloater's burst that killed the player
    mid-blow let one of the dead bite again in the same step (two deaths, the last card twice); the
    falling body was never drawn (its clock a moment ahead of the frame's); the low-health pulse
    outlived the city, and hid a bite's flash; a brute's and a bloater's lit eyes floated over their
    heads; a fallen civilian lost its colours. **Found while fixing:** the pulse's animation bore the
    name of the meters' own and had replaced it.
  - **Accepted:** a bloater's burst is by distance alone (no wall stops it); the tip says to strike
    from a spear's full reach.
  - **Not checked:** the looks of six of the eleven were seen in the browser, a body lying where it
    fell once; a real phone's speed with a dozen draws for the dead; the sounds were
    not listened to; a real pad, touch; the French by a native speaker.
- **City Escape: what its design promised and the build left out (v2.61.0)**, the
  user's request (2026-11-16: "great, yes let's go with section 1 and 2", the design's gaps and the
  known limits of 2.58 to 2.60, offered as two sections).
  - **Choices (Claude's, told to the user; the numbers in `content/dead.ts` and `sim/tools.ts`):** the
    design's own rules, not new ones. A bite bleeds and a bandage stops it; the dead only go down to a
    blow without an edge and get up unless finished; a chained building's window is the loud way in
    beside the crowbar's quiet one; the boat's fog, a rule since 2.48, is drawn; the other four off the
    bus get a note each. Bleeding never takes the last point, as food never kills (2.58).
  - **Built, the rules:** `state.bleed` (half a bite's harm, sixteen at most, a point every two
    seconds, stopped by a bandage or a medkit at any health, gone at a fall); `Zed.down` (a blunt blow
    that empties one leaves it lying eight seconds where it stood, harmless, deaf; then up after the
    player with a third of its health; any blow, bolt, fire or burst finishes it; a knife, a cleaver or
    a spear never only knocks down, nor is a crawler or a bloater knocked down; the blow takes one on its
    feet first, and the blow after a knockdown comes in 3 steps, not 12: followed through); a target `pane` (`x` + tile) on the glass of a building still chained, broken from the
    street in 1.2 s, heard 14 tiles off, the building forced; bare hands cut, `state.panes` kept for the
    picture; a bloater's burst stopped by a wall (`lineOfSight`); four notes (`BUS_NOTES`, `busNotes`)
    placed after everything else from a stream of their own, so a saved city keeps its chains and its
    loot; the reader feat is every note.
  - **Built, the picture and the screens:** where windows are and what fills them is one list
    (`content/windows.ts`) the facades and the rules read; a broken one is a dark hole, a tooth of glass,
    shards outside; one lying knocked down is drawn on its back, twitching before it gets up; the docks'
    fog on the boat's night (a bank over the heads of the dead, clear round the player, drifting); the
    survivors' lanterns lit in their glass, their pool an amber wash laid over the floor (added to it, a
    pale floor washed out to white); the health meter drips while bleeding; toasts, two tips, the
    fight's, the dead's, the tools' and the story's pages brought up to date.
  - **Measured:** the scavenger bot, 100 cities a week each: 1.82 falls a week before, 2.29 with the
    rules first built (bleeding off 2.09, blades everywhere 2.26, a lighter bleed no better), 1.91 with
    the blow followed through. The holds with a bat and three bandages, 32 cities: night 1 the helicopter
    31 to 32, the metro 32 to 27; night 5 the helicopter 30 to 25, the metro 27 to 21 (with blades
    everywhere 27 and 26: the knockdown's third blow is the cost; a longer or weaker rise changed nothing,
    bodies walked over or the dropped one finished first made it worse). Of 2,172 panes of chained
    buildings, 1,553 are glass. A burst behind a wall: 6 of 6 clear.
  - **Moved snapshots:** `citytest` (the state's fingerprint: its new fields alone, the same hash with
    them taken out), `cityzedtest`, `cityfighttest`, `citytooltest`, `citystorytest` (their new lines; the
    bots' runs), `citysurvivetest` (falls 2.3 to 1.6 a week on its ten cities), `helptest`, `i18ntest`.
  - **Found by the independent review, fixed:** the docks' fog stayed in the scene after the city (a boat's
    escape, a quit); one lying knocked down still counted as a chaser, so the pack waited behind it for
    eight seconds and the HUD said two were after the player; the window the button meant was never
    framed; a bolt or a bottle went to one lying nearer before one on its feet; a body finished where it
    lay stood up to fall again; the fog veiled the story's marks; the bleeding meter's word stayed in the
    old language. The facades drawn as before: 63,389 parts, the same hash, old code and new.
  - **Accepted:** the endings' last night is harder with a bat (see Measured): a blade, a bolt or fire
    finishes, which is the design's point; the user decides whether the holds are shortened.
  - **Found in the browser:** the fog's bank under the heads of the dead (raised over them); the
    lantern's flame hidden in its own glass; the tip spoke of a bite when glass had cut.
  - **Seen in the browser:** the eleven looks of the dead, each in its district; the Intake over the
    city at 1280×760 and 412×915 (it reads: the prisoner beside the bus, the transport list's card).
  - **Not checked:** played to an end by a person; a real phone, pad and listening; the French by a
    native speaker; a window broken by touch.
