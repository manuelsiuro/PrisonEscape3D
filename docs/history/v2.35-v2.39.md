# History: v2.35 to v2.39

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser, and what was accepted as it is. Split from `v2.30-v2.39.md` on 2026-11-07, word for word, when that file reached its 80 KB; from 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **The phone performance pass (v2.35.0)**, the fifth of the queue in `BACKLOG.md`
  §4 (2026-11-06: "start session 5"; the user's picks: a frame counter for real phones, savings that
  change no pixel, a test that guards it; no lighter look; the user reads the counter on their phone).
  - **Measured first** (`scripts/dev/perfprobe.ts`, new: a phone held upright, the phone's tier, the
    CPU slowed 4×; six prisons at waking, roll call, lunch, the yard, the evening and night): 60 fps
    in all 36 scenes, the frame's script 2.2–5.3 ms (its slowest 5% under 7.5 ms), 49–116 draw calls
    and 20–63 in the shadow pass (`BACKLOG.md`'s 166–254 was v2.5.0's), 110k–275k triangles. The
    graphics card is the unknown: this computer's (an M4 Pro) takes 1.5–1.9 ms a frame, a third of it
    the shadows and most of the rest by pixel; a phone's may be 10–20 times slower.
  - **Built:** the frame counter (`app/perf.ts`, `perfCounter.ts` fetched only when on): `?perf` in
    the address turns it on and remembers it (`pe3d.perf`), `?perf=0` off; bottom left above the item
    bar, every half second the frames a second, the frame's time and its slowest 5%, the graphics
    card's time where the browser has a timer, the tier, the draws, triangles and pixels, the card's
    name; English and French. The trees round a prison (280, one instanced mesh) and San Pancho's
    minefield (1,024 mounds, 421 lids, never culled) split into squares of 16 tiles the camera and
    the shadow pass skip out of sight (`render/chunks.ts`). `perfprobe`'s `check()` holds 36 scenes'
    calls and triangles to `perfbudget.json` (a sixth over fails).
  - **Proved:** triangles a frame San Pancho 258k–275k → 77k–98k, the Training Block 110k → 55k,
    Center Perks and Stalag Flucht about 40% fewer, Shankton 15–20%; Irongate as it was (no trees
    round its island); draw calls the same (±4), 60 fps everywhere. `drawhash`'s 258 frames identical
    to the old code's. The counter seen at 412×915 and 915×412 in both languages, `?perf=0` checked.
  - **Found:** `drawhash` moved with the split though no pixel did: three.js draws each object's id
    from `Math.random`, so 47 more objects shifted the seeded stream behind the characters' blinks
    (proved: the old code with as many empty objects matched 174 frames, and the split and one mesh
    compared pixel for pixel matched zoomed out). Ids now draw from a stream of their own, the
    baseline retaken on the old code. A general split of the walls and floors was measured and left:
    on this card hiding 18k triangles saved 0.1 ms, and the walls are shown and hidden by index.
  - **Found by the review** (one independent reviewer; the minefield old and new built side by side,
    every mound and lid the same through six days of craters): with the counter on, `perfprobe`'s
    time query nested in the counter's and its graphics times stayed empty until a reload (one query
    at a time now, the counter's set aside while it measures); a disjoint read once a result let
    spoiled times in (once a poll, every waiting one dropped); the minefield never freed its instance
    buffers itself (older than this; `dispose()` on each piece now).
  - **Accepted:** `check()` runs by hand in the browser, like `drawhash`, not in CI (no browser there).
    Irongate's sea is one 66k-triangle mesh, drawn whole. One hitch seen (Shankton 15:05, a few slow
    frames): session 6's subject.
  - **On a real phone** (the user's suggestion, 2026-11-06: a Galaxy S24, Exynos 2400, Xclipse 940,
    on USB: `adb reverse` to the dev server, `perfprobe` run through Chrome's remote debugging): 60 fps
    and no late frame in all 36 scenes at the phone's tier (540×961), the frame's script 5.8–7.9 ms
    (its slowest 5% under 12 ms), 48–115 calls; every tier holds 60 fps in Irongate's and
    Shankton's busiest scenes, the screen's full 1080×1923 with the best shadows too. No graphics
    timer there (the counter says so). The counter cut the card's name short on Android: the names
    are now read for ANGLE on Metal, Vulkan, Qualcomm and Direct3D (`cardName`).
  - **Then** (the user's pick, 2026-11-06: "commit, merge and push, then create the skill"): the
    `real-phone` skill and `scripts/dev/phone.ts` (setup, code run in the phone's tab, its screen, real
    touches by text, element or point; Chrome's install sheet closed first, as it took the touches meant
    for the item bar). Proved on the S24: the Tutorial opened by a tap, lesson 1's first step passed by
    a held slide out of the cell.
  - **Then** (the user's request, 2026-11-06: "add a section (developper) in the settings panel to
    enable/disable the perf card"): Settings ends with a Developer section, its switch for the frame
    counter (`setPerf`: at once, remembered in the same `pe3d.perf` as `?perf`; the counter taken down
    with its waiting time queries); the counter now under the dialogs (z 45: Settings covered it).
    Played on the S24 by real taps, in French: on, the counter up at once, off, gone and forgotten.
    Found doing it: a tap aimed at a button below the page's fold landed on Android's Back and
    brought another app of the user's to the front (nothing in it was touched). `phone.ts` now
    scrolls an element into view first, refuses a touch off the page or on a covered element, and
    sends none unless Chrome is the app in front.
  - **Not checked:** a mid-range or older phone, where the graphics card could be the limit
    (`BACKLOG.md` §2); the S24's 120 Hz (its Chrome ran at 60).

- **The design tokens (v2.35.0)**, the seventh of the queue in `BACKLOG.md` §4
  (2026-11-06: "start session 7"; the user's pick: tokens with the same look, twins merged, a picture's

    `--kiosk-gold-85`); the existing tokens (`--gold`, `--ok`, `--steel`…) reused, never `--ink`,

    alpha where it's used (`rgba(var(--gold-rgb), 0.45)`), so no alpha moved and session 9 can swap
