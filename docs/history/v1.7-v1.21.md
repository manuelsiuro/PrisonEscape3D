# History: v1.7 to v1.21

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser, and what was accepted as it is. Split from `docs/HISTORY.md` by version on 2026-11-06, word for word; `HISTORY.md` is the index.

- **The new version, "Prison Life" (decided 2026-10-16)**: the user asked for fun features beyond the clone and chose all of them: noise and distractions, daily events and weather, guard dogs and a pet rat, guards with personalities, mood speech bubbles ("varied, never repetitive or linear"), new ways out (stowaway, fake-ID bluff, glider), smuggling, crew specialists, side games, achievements and medals, your prisoner, escape replays and a Daily Escape. Everywhere in the campaign, tuned: the 18 escape bots keep winning, byte for byte. One release at a time (P13 Voices → P14 Noise → P15 Guard ways → P16 Achievements & you → P17 The day → P18 Stowaway → P19 Dogs → P20 Rat → P21 Smuggling → P22 Games → P23 Crew → P24 Bluff & glider → P25 Replays → P26 Daily Escape as v2.0).
- **P13 Voices (v1.7.0)**: speech bubbles over inmates and guards (see §2): HTML over the 3D view (no draw call), at most 4 on a desktop, 3 on a phone held sideways, 2 upright, never over the HUD or the controls (measured), nudged apart; the director picks who speaks by voice, mood and distance, a fitting line not heard lately (now and then from another mood that fits the moment), and stays silent rather than repeat. 1,000 lines a language in 174 banks; 21 kinds of gossip, each checked against the running game in every prison (bed checks observed, the generator switched off, a tunnel dug past its braces, guards floored until the takeover, a bed check with and without a dummy). Guard names on the target card ("Officer Pratt · cyan key"); the inmate's greeting in the talk panel; a Rumors section in the file. The bed checks' times moved into `tuning.ts` (`BED_CHECK_MINUTES`) so the gossip quotes the rule's own numbers. Measured: 60 fps under a 4× CPU slowdown with bubbles up; two days of play say about 300 lines, 91%+ distinct in any 100, none again among the last 40.

- **P14 Noise & distractions (v1.8.0)**: guards hear the player's work and anything thrown, by the rules in §2: a noise spreads tile by tile where sight would pass (a shut door costs 4, walls stop it; a wall being chipped is heard on both sides), from below only on the 3×3 tiles above, from the vents within 2 tiles below, from a roof on the open ground around (off the ground floor a tile's cost is its distance across and down, so the guards sent walk towards it); the guards awake within earshot hear it, the nearest go and look where it came from (`src/sim/systems/noise.ts`: `makeNoise`, `hearers`, `wouldHear`, `strikeNoise`, `aimToss`, `throwItem`, `noisemakers` first in the step). Throwing (`OP.EXT`, the first of the shared extension ops: `EXT.THROW`, the ground tile aimed at in its two arguments, so a throw sent while walking lands where it was aimed; commands gained a third 6-bit argument): on a mouse, hold the right button to aim and let go (or Throw in the item's menu, then click; F straight ahead); on touch, Throw in the item's menu (or the firecracker's own button), drag and lift. The aim shows the reach, a dotted arc, the landing ring, the tiles the thud would reach and an ear over each guard who would hear it; a label says who would (or why it can't go), kept clear of the hint and the screen's edges. Lands at once (the view flies it, the item waits for it to land), spills off a full pile, −2 opinion with an inmate it hits, never scanned over an arch. The firecracker lands lit (`state.fuses`, saved) and goes off 1.5 s later: a bang that wakes the guards asleep within earshot and sends three. The alarm clock is wound with the use button (20 s a press, a minute at most; a press stops it ringing), counts down wherever it is (its slot holds its state: in the pockets, furniture, someone's pockets, the floor) and rings once a second for 8 s; its countdown shows on its slot, updated in place (a redraw every second would eat clicks). An ear on the tool button (and the Space prompt) when a guard would hear the work. Waves and the reached tiles flash where a noise is made; a "?" over each guard sent to look; sounds (clatter soft/hard/metal, the fuse, the bang, the ring, a tick in the pockets, winding); bubble lines for guards and inmates (8 new banks). A lesson, "Noise" (tutorial 9 of 10); a lesson step can now end the lesson when it can't be done any more (both firecrackers gone off out of earshot: try again). An independent review found 10 bugs before release (the lesson's dead end, the hint over the phone buttons, a throw on the move landing a tile on, the label under the hint or the item bar, toasts over the hint, guards stalling on a roof or vent noise, clicks lost to the clock's redraws, the ear left on the attack button, a lit fuse behind the title, ticking ×10 while out cold): all fixed, the lesson's and the throw's with tests. Escape bots unchanged; at most +4 draw calls while aiming; 60 fps at a 4× CPU slowdown.
- **P15 Guard ways (v1.9.0)**: guards have their ways (`GuardDef.trait`, §2): steady, lazy, strict, corrupt, set per prison (Center Perks: Pratt corrupt, Dunn strict, Moss lazy, napping in the yard). Talking to a guard (`s.talk` holds a guard's Cast id; `canChat`): it stands still facing the player; its panel shows its name, its key, its ways once a rumor told them, a greeting in its voice, and two bribes (`TALK.BRIBE`, `src/sim/systems/ways.ts`): look the other way (2 game hours; `watch.ts` lets the hour, staff areas, contraband and a uniform pass under its eyes, and its sight doesn't count as seen) and lose the paperwork (−25 heat, once a day); a strict guard reports the offer (+20 heat), the others say no; refused at 80% heat, a look away off at 80%. A HUD chip counts down the look away; the bribed guard's cone turns pale green. Gossip tells each guard's ways (and where and from when a lazy one naps) and which cells the next shakedown will search, all checked by `gossiptest` against the game (each guard offered a bribe, the naps and shakedowns that happened). Guard voices by trait (slacker, stickler, crook; steady guards stay rookies or veterans), 15 new banks (dozing, bribed, greetings, a guard woken, bribes taken, refused, reported). Help topic "Guard ways"; coach hints for talking to a guard and for the first dozing one seen. An independent review found 8 issues before release, all fixed: a dozing guard could be beaten out cold without waking (a blow wakes it now, a knockout ends the nap); a guard's ways fell out of the notebook as daily shakedown news came in (lasting rumors are always kept, news capped at 12); the look away's countdown redrew the panel every second and disarmed the paperwork button (now written in place); dozing guards shouted reactions; a talk could hold a guard past its block (it ends when its duty changes); the paperwork limit is once a day per guard (said so); a strict shakedown's last search may run into breakfast (documented). Escape bots unchanged; 184 draw calls, 60 fps.
