# History: v2.50 to v2.54

Every release in this range, in order: what changed, what the reviews found and fixed, what was found in the browser,
and what was accepted as it is. From 2.35 a file holds five minor versions.

- **City Escape: the landmarks' own models (v2.50.0)**, the user's request (2026-11-13:
  "continue with the landmarks' own models in a new branch"), parked in `BACKLOG.md` §4b since 2.49.0.
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (the finish's row): render only, nothing where
    anyone walks; pictograms and no words on the signs (one model for both languages).
  - **Built:** `city/render/landmarkPlan.ts` (pure: where each piece goes, from the district's picture and
    the story's places) and `city/render/landmarks.ts`. Every landmark: a coping in its colour along the
    tops of its walls, a board with its pictogram tipped up to the camera on the north wall, and its own
    things on its corners (the tower's lattice masts and dishes, the factory's chimneys and tank, the
    rangers' lookout, the fuel station's totem, the mall's flags and rooftop units, the police's lamps and
    aerial, the metro's roundels, the hospital's windsock and beacon mast, the boathouse's pennants). The
    ways out: a helipad painted on the hospital's clear floor nearest the beacon's tile, the beacon's lamp
    flashing once lit and the helicopter coming in over the last fourteen game minutes of the hold
    (`Sound.rotor`); a boat moored on the water beside the pier's end, its outboard and fuel drum aboard
    once the engine is in; the stairs down at the metro's way out under a grille that slides aside when
    the gates open. The still pieces are one merged model of their own, cut away round
    the player as the walls are (one draw call, about 1,500 triangles); `LandmarkLive` holds the few
    that change (at most five small meshes, in their own district only).
  - **Not touched:** the simulation, the generator, saves: `tilesOf` is exported from `sim/story.ts`,
    nothing else; the city's six other tests' snapshots are unchanged.
  - **Found in the browser:** a small pad under the player's ring read as nothing (the pad moved to the
    nearest clear three-by-three); the boat's blue line was a lid (a rim); mooring posts doubled the
    pier's own (dropped); masts too slight from this camera (taller, sturdier); the grille slid onto a
    bench, which isn't solid (clear floor now means no furniture at all).
  - **An independent review** (the diff; placement probed on 200 cities), eight findings, all fixed: the
    helicopter and the lit beacon vanished behind the last card at the win (kept once left by
    helicopter); the sign, the south wall's coping and the lookout hid the player, not being cut away as
    walls are (a model of their own on a see-through material); the mall's rooftop units placed blind,
    on the sign in 44 cities of 200 and over a door in 22 (places chosen in the plan, clear of both; the
    police's light bars too); the grille slid onto a doorway in 2 of 200 (clear floor is a floor); its
    speed followed the frame rate (by time); a survivor on the pad in 3 of 100 (their tiles kept clear);
    the docs' small slips; the test's gaps (`LandmarkLive` built and drawn, the sign's legs, the rooftop
    places).
  - **Proof:** `citylandmarktest` (24 cities, 216 landmarks: the coping, corners, sign and rooftop pieces
    on walls, the pad and the grille on clear floor, the boat on water; the still model builds and keeps
    near its building; the changing pieces put up, drawn through the story's states and taken down).
  - **Not checked:** a real phone's speed with the landmark in view (the triangles are few and merged);
    the police's, the mall's and the fuel station's crowns were built to the same pattern as the six looked
    at in the browser, and not each looked at; the pad's small form and a grille with nowhere to slide never
    came up in 200 cities (drawn by rule only); reduced motion by its rule in the code, not on screen.

- **City Escape: music of its own (v2.51.0)**, the user's request (2026-11-13: "continue
  with music of its own in a new branch"), parked in `BACKLOG.md` §4b since 2.49.0.
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (the finish's row): a key of its own (E Phrygian,
    against the prisons' D minor), five moods, picked from what is happening, synthesized like the rest.
  - **Built:** `audio/cityScore.ts` (one progression, Em F Dm Em: `cityDay` a drone and a lonely plucked
    phrase; `cityNight` the drone, a cluster a semitone wide, a slow heartbeat, struck metal far off;
    `cityShelter` the same notes on their major side with a music box; `cityChase` drums on every beat
    and a biting ostinato; `cityHold` the chase's drive under a climbing arpeggio), played through
    `SoundMusic`'s own voices (`Mood` grows by five). `city/music.ts` picks the mood each frame: the last
    stand first, then being chased (lingering four seconds after the last of them gives up), then the
    hour, and at night whether the player is behind a barred door or in a survivor's building.
  - **Measured** on the real engine in the browser (an analyser on the music's output, nine seconds a
    mood): the three quiet moods at the prisons' day and night level; the chase and the hold came out
    louder than the tension theme and were brought down to it (RMS 0.0077 and 0.0068 against 0.0066).
    In a city under way the mood went day, night, chase as its causes were set.
  - **Proof:** `citymusictest` (64 bars a mood: every pitch within a detuned saw's width of the scale,
    the struck metal's partials aside; nothing over the prisons' score's loudest; no two silent bars; the
    picking rule walked through a night). It found a partial at 6.3 kHz (the metal, an octave lower now).
  - **An independent review** (the diff; a probe of 256 bars a mood), seven findings, all fixed: the night
    was all but silent on a phone (its drone, fifth and heartbeat under 70 Hz: a dark thin chord and the
    fifth two octaves up added; the level figures above are broadband); the chase played four seconds
    over a Survivor's waking at home (forgotten at a fall); the boat lying ready put the last stand's
    music on every night in the docks (only while the tide is in: it is the quiet way out); the fast
    moods at full level under the pause and the cards (ducked; the shelter's mood once out, the night's
    once fallen for good); the fourth chord's pad had no E (it has); the test's claims tightened (what
    a small speaker plays, partials only with a tuned note, a fall in the walk).
  - **Not checked:** nobody has listened to it: whether it is good music is for the user's ears
    (`BACKLOG.md` §2, with every other sound).

- **City Escape: light and fire (v2.52.0)**, the user's request (2026-11-14: "continue with
  light and fire in a new branch"), parked in `BACKLOG.md` §4b since 2.47.0; the design's §4.3.
  - **Choices:** Claude's picks, in `decisions/city.md` (a row of its own): everything lit from the
    pockets (Use), so the action button's order is untouched; strong light keeps back only the dead that
    aren't after the player; a brute and the last stand's called ones ignore it; the torch is a dim
    light, seen further behind it.
  - **Built:** `content/light.ts`, `sim/light.ts`. A **fire barrel** (fuel used beside a street barrel:
    four tiles round it with a clear line, eight game hours, and it cooks as the camp stove does), a
    **lantern** (a new item and a twelfth recipe: a bottle, cloth, fuel; set down where the player stands:
    three tiles, ten hours), a **generator** (fuel used beside one: its whole building lit twelve hours),
    the **torch** (a battery lasts four hours; on, the player is seen at the day's six tiles and not the
    night's four; off in bed). In the lit tiles (`City.lit`) the dead that aren't chasing are not put
    down, don't pick a place to go, turn back at the edge, and leave when a light is lit round them. The
    state grows by `torch`, `torchOn`, `lights` (optional on load, made sound before the city is built).
    On screen: the lit tiles washed warm (exactly the tiles the rule covers), flames, a lantern, a work
    lamp, the torch's wider pool; at night the dead beyond the player's light are shapes and eyes; a
    pill under the goal's line (in the light, by the torch with its time left, in the dark); a help topic
    (`cityLight`), a first tip, `Sound.ignite`.
  - **Old cities:** a city with nothing burning plays as before: the five bots' runs are the same step
    for step (their snapshots moved only by the state's fingerprint, three new fields, and the recipe
    list's twelfth line).
  - **Measured** (`citylighttest`): a first night asleep in an open house, 12 cities: bitten on 3 in the
    dark, on 1 beside a lantern; the dead caught in a lantern's light gone in 14 steps; four indoors out
    of a generator's building in 71.
  - **An independent review** (the diff; five probes), nine findings, all fixed: a fall's jump to morning
    left burnt-out lights in the lit map until the next minute, so a save made then put the morning's
    dead down elsewhere (relit after the jump); a light stayed lit up to 19 steps past its end (it ends
    on a minute's turn); a lantern by the bed was a whole shelter wherever no brute comes, nothing ever
    seeing the sleeper (asleep in strong light they are seen at the night's four tiles, not two); the
    view's read of the lights wrote an empty list into the state (read only); the thirteenth light put
    out the oldest unsaid (said); a save's light could burn for ever (capped); a barrel cornerwise took
    no fuel though it cooked (it does); two checks of the test that proved nothing and "738 bites
    against none", three nights of twelve counted bite by bite (rewritten: nights bitten).
  - **Accepted:** the three small shapes the lights are drawn from are freed and sent again to the
    graphics card whenever the lights change (a few bytes); one of the dead that loses the player inside
    a lit building by day may stand idle in it.
  - **Not checked:** touch, a real pad, a real phone; the fire barrel, the generator and the torch's
    pool were looked at less than the lantern (the generator's lamp not at all on screen); French on
    screen (read in the file); `Sound.ignite` not listened to.
  - **Found in the browser:** the pool too faint to read and the picture's place too cramped (stronger, a
    hot middle; staged where a lantern lights most); the light pill ran into the corner map on a phone (a
    row of its own); the fire took the radio's crackle (its own sound); `cityDead`'s picture retaken (the
    night's shapes).
